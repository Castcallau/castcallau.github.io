Common questions

What is the best bait for Coral Trout?

The best baits for Coral Trout are Whole pilchard, Live fusilier / hardiheads, Squid. Lures that work: 5–7" soft plastics, Metal and slow-pitch jigs, Deep-diving minnows trolled along the reef edge.

When is the best time to catch Coral Trout?

Coral Trout are at their best in Apr–Oct (good from Jan–Dec). They bite best dawn and dusk and around the tide change, in water around 22–29°C.

Where can you catch Coral Trout?

Coral reefs, bommies and rubble edges on the Great Barrier Reef and Ningaloo. Top spots on CastCall include Whitsundays, Great Barrier Reef, Exmouth.

What is the legal size for Coral Trout?

Check current NSW DPI rules. Size and bag limits differ between states and change over time, so always check the current rules before keeping fish.
