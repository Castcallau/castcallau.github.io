Best baits for Spotted & Spanish Mackerel

  • Live slimy mackerel / yakka
  • Whole garfish or pilchard trolled slowly

Best lures

  • Metal slugs 40–80g cranked fast
  • Bibbed minnows trolled
  • High-speed spinning

Rig

Short wire trace (they have razor teeth), 4/0–7/0 hooks or ganged rig.

Size and bag limit

Check current NSW DPI rules. Limits differ between states – always check your state's current rules.

Common questions

What is the best bait for Spotted & Spanish Mackerel?

The best baits for Spotted & Spanish Mackerel are Live slimy mackerel / yakka, Whole garfish or pilchard trolled slowly. Lures that work: Metal slugs 40–80g cranked fast, Bibbed minnows trolled, High-speed spinning.

When is the best time to catch Spotted & Spanish Mackerel?

Spotted & Spanish Mackerel are at their best in Feb–Apr (good from Jan–May). They bite best dawn and around the tide change, in water around 22–29°C.

Where can you catch Spotted & Spanish Mackerel?

North Coast headlands, inshore reefs and bait schools (Coffs Harbour and north). Top spots on CastCall include South West Rocks, Coffs Harbour, Solitary Islands, Ballina, Byron Bay, Moreton Bay.

What is the legal size for Spotted & Spanish Mackerel?

Check current NSW DPI rules. Size and bag limits differ between states and change over time, so always check the current rules before keeping fish.
