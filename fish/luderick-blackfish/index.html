Best baits for Luderick

  • Green weed (tidal ribbon weed)
  • Cabbage weed (on the rocks)
  • Squirt worms

Best lures

  • Not a lure fish – weed or cabbage only

Rig

Stemmed float with split shot to sit the bait just above the weed, #8–#10 hook, 6lb line. Berley with chopped weed and sand.

Size and bag limit

NSW: min 27 cm, bag 20 (guide only). Limits differ between states – always check your state's current rules.

Common questions

What is the best bait for Luderick?

The best baits for Luderick are Green weed (tidal ribbon weed), Cabbage weed (on the rocks), Squirt worms. Lures that work: Not a lure fish – weed or cabbage only.

When is the best time to catch Luderick?

Luderick are at their best in Apr–Aug (good from Mar–Oct). They bite best during the day and on any tide, in water around 14–22°C.

Where can you catch Luderick?

Weed beds, breakwalls, rock washes, bridge pylons. Top spots on CastCall include Port Hacking, Long Reef Headland, Narrabeen Lake Entrance, The Entrance, Norah Head Rocks, Newcastle.

What is the legal size for Luderick?

NSW: min 27 cm, bag 20 (guide only). Size and bag limits differ between states and change over time, so always check the current rules before keeping fish.
