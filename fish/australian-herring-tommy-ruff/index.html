Best baits for Australian Herring

  • Maggots / wogs under a float
  • Prawn pieces
  • Squid strips
  • Berley (bread and pollard)

Best lures

  • Small "herring poppers" with a trailing jig
  • Small metal slices

Rig

Light gear: small float, #10–#8 hook, 4–6lb line, steady berley trail.

Common questions

What is the best bait for Australian Herring?

The best baits for Australian Herring are Maggots / wogs under a float, Prawn pieces, Squid strips, Berley (bread and pollard). Lures that work: Small "herring poppers" with a trailing jig, Small metal slices.

When is the best time to catch Australian Herring?

Australian Herring are at their best in Apr–Jun (good from Mar–Aug). They bite best dawn and dusk and on any tide, in water around 14–22°C.

Where can you catch Australian Herring?

Jetties, beaches, rock groynes and estuary mouths in WA and SA. Top spots on CastCall include Swan River, Perth Beaches, Rottnest Island, Mandurah Estuary, Busselton Jetty, Albany.

What is the legal size for Australian Herring?

Check current NSW DPI rules. Size and bag limits differ between states and change over time, so always check the current rules before keeping fish.
