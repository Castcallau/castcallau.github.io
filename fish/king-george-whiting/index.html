Common questions

What is the best bait for King George Whiting?

The best baits for King George Whiting are Pipis, Squid strips, Mussel, Sandworm / bloodworm. Lures that work: Small soft plastics or blades hopped along the sand.

When is the best time to catch King George Whiting?

King George Whiting are at their best in Nov–Apr (good from Sep–Jun). They bite best during the day and on the rising (run-in) tide, in water around 14–22°C.

Where can you catch King George Whiting?

Sand patches between seagrass beds in bays and gulfs of VIC, SA and southern WA. Top spots on CastCall include Rottnest Island, Busselton Jetty, Albany, Esperance, Adelaide Jetties, Port Lincoln.

What is the legal size for King George Whiting?

Check current NSW DPI rules. Size and bag limits differ between states and change over time, so always check the current rules before keeping fish.
