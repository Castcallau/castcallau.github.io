Best lures

  • Small soft plastics
  • Micro jigs
  • Small metal slices

Rig

Light running sinker or unweighted bait, #1–2/0 hook, 8–10lb leader. Berley keeps them around.

Common questions

What is the best bait for Silver Trevally?

The best baits for Silver Trevally are Peeled prawn, Pilchard pieces, Squid strips, Bread / tuna cubes in berley. Lures that work: Small soft plastics, Micro jigs, Small metal slices.

When is the best time to catch Silver Trevally?

Silver Trevally are at their best in May–Aug (good from Mar–Oct). They bite best dawn and dusk and on the rising (run-in) tide, in water around 14–22°C.

Where can you catch Silver Trevally?

Harbours, bays, wharves and deeper estuary channels. Top spots on CastCall include Kurnell, Clifton Gardens Wharf, Forster–Tuncurry Breakwall, Coffs Harbour, Ballina, Wollongong Harbour & Breakwall.

What is the legal size for Silver Trevally?

Check current NSW DPI rules. Size and bag limits differ between states and change over time, so always check the current rules before keeping fish.
