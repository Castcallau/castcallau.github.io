Best baits for Australian Bass

  • Live shrimp
  • Crickets / cicadas
  • Scrub worms
  • Small live poddy mullet

Best lures

  • Surface fizzers / poppers at dawn, dusk and night
  • Small spinnerbaits
  • Lipless crankbaits
  • 2–3" soft plastics

Rig

6–10lb braid with a 10–12lb leader. Unweighted live bait cast tight to snags; lures pitched into cover.

Check current NSW DPI rules. Limits differ between states – always check your state's current rules.

Common questions

What is the best bait for Australian Bass?

The best baits for Australian Bass are Live shrimp, Crickets / cicadas, Scrub worms, Small live poddy mullet. Lures that work: Surface fizzers / poppers at dawn, dusk and night, Small spinnerbaits, Lipless crankbaits, 2–3" soft plastics.

When is the best time to catch Australian Bass?

Australian Bass are at their best in Oct–Mar (good from Sep–Apr). They bite best dawn and dusk and on any tide, in water around 16–28°C.

Where can you catch Australian Bass?

Snags, overhanging trees and rocky banks in coastal rivers; weed edges and points in stocked dams. Top spots on CastCall include Nepean River, Lake Lyell, Lake St Clair, Glenbawn Dam, Clarence River.

What is the legal size for Australian Bass?

Check current NSW DPI rules. Size and bag limits differ between states and change over time, so always check the current rules before keeping fish.
