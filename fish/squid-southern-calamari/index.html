Best baits for Squid

  • Pilchard / mullet on a squid spike
  • Live yellowtail on a squid jag

Best lures

  • Egi squid jigs 2.5–3.5 (natural colours by day, orange/pink at dusk)

Rig

Squid jig on a 1–1.5 m 10–15lb fluoro leader. Cast, let it sink, two sharp lifts and a long pause.

Common questions

What is the best bait for Squid?

The best baits for Squid are Pilchard / mullet on a squid spike, Live yellowtail on a squid jag. Lures that work: Egi squid jigs 2.5–3.5 (natural colours by day, orange/pink at dusk).

When is the best time to catch Squid?

Squid are at their best in May–Oct (good from Jan–Dec). They bite best dawn and dusk and on any tide, in water around 14–22°C.

Where can you catch Squid?

Clear water over weed beds and sand patches in harbours, bays and off rocks. Top spots on CastCall include Kurnell, Clifton Gardens Wharf, Middle Harbour, Port Hacking, Hawkesbury River, Pittwater.

What is the legal size for Squid?

Check current NSW DPI rules. Size and bag limits differ between states and change over time, so always check the current rules before keeping fish.
