Common questions

What is the best bait for Australian Bonito?

The best baits for Australian Bonito are Whole pilchard, Live yakka. Lures that work: Metal slugs 20–40g, Small high-speed minnows.

When is the best time to catch Australian Bonito?

Australian Bonito are at their best in Apr–Jun (good from Mar–Jul). They bite best dawn and on any tide, in water around 16–22°C.

Where can you catch Australian Bonito?

Off the rocks, harbour entrances and inshore reefs chasing bait. Top spots on CastCall include Sydney Offshore Reefs, Broughton Island, Bass Point, Montague Island, Bate Bay.

What is the legal size for Australian Bonito?

Check current NSW DPI rules. Size and bag limits differ between states and change over time, so always check the current rules before keeping fish.
