Best baits for Australian Salmon

  • Pilchard on gang hooks
  • Beach worms
  • Peeled prawn
  • Bluebait

Best lures

  • Metal slugs 20–60g
  • 3–5" soft plastic minnows
  • Poppers when schools are surface feeding

Rig

Gang hooks or a paternoster rig with a sinker on the bottom in the surf. 15–20lb line.

Size and bag limit

NSW: min 30 cm, bag 5 (guide only). Limits differ between states – always check your state's current rules.

Common questions

What is the best bait for Australian Salmon?

The best baits for Australian Salmon are Pilchard on gang hooks, Beach worms, Peeled prawn, Bluebait. Lures that work: Metal slugs 20–60g, 3–5" soft plastic minnows, Poppers when schools are surface feeding.

When is the best time to catch Australian Salmon?

Australian Salmon are at their best in Apr–Aug (good from Mar–Sep). They bite best dawn and dusk and on any tide, in water around 13–20°C.

Where can you catch Australian Salmon?

Surf beach gutters, rock platforms, harbour and estuary mouths. Top spots on CastCall include Long Reef Headland, Maroubra Beach, Norah Head Rocks, Newcastle, Wollongong Harbour & Breakwall, Bass Point.

What is the legal size for Australian Salmon?

NSW: min 30 cm, bag 5 (guide only). Size and bag limits differ between states and change over time, so always check the current rules before keeping fish.
