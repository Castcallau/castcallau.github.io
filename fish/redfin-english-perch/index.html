Rig

Light gear, 4–6lb. Paternoster rig with worms, or small lures jigged straight down on a school.

Size and bag limit

Pest – no size or bag limit, don’t release alive. Limits differ between states – always check your state's current rules.

Common questions

What is the best bait for Redfin?

The best baits for Redfin are Scrub worms, Small yabbies, Live shrimp. Lures that work: Small soft plastics, Inline spinners, Ice jigs / blades jigged vertically.

When is the best time to catch Redfin?

Redfin are at their best in Mar–Apr, Oct–Nov (good from Sep–May). They bite best dawn and dusk and on any tide, in water around 12–24°C.

Where can you catch Redfin?

Schools around weed beds, drowned timber and drop-offs in dams and slow rivers. Top spots on CastCall include Lake Lyell, Lake Burrendong, Windamere Dam, Burrinjuck Dam, Lake Mulwala, Murray River.

What is the legal size for Redfin?

Pest – no size or bag limit, don’t release alive. Size and bag limits differ between states and change over time, so always check the current rules before keeping fish.
