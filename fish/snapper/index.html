Snapper fishing: best bait, lures, rigs & where to catch them

Chrysophrys auratus · Inshore and offshore reefs, gravel beds, washy rocky headlands. Low-light, a bit of swell and a falling barometer are perfect. Big reds move in close to the washes after a southerly blow.

Best baits for Snapper

  • Pilchard (unweighted floater)
  • Squid or cuttlefish strips
  • Fresh yakka / slimy mackerel fillet
  • Prawns

Best lures

  • 5–7" soft plastic jerk shads
  • Micro jigs / slow-pitch jigs
  • Vibes

Rig

Floater rig: two snelled 4/0–5/0 hooks with the smallest sinker you can get away with, drifted back in a berley trail.

Size and bag limit

NSW: min 30 cm, bag 10 (guide only). Limits differ between states – always check your state's current rules.

Common questions

What is the best bait for Snapper?

The best baits for Snapper are Pilchard (unweighted floater), Squid or cuttlefish strips, Fresh yakka / slimy mackerel fillet, Prawns. Lures that work: 5–7" soft plastic jerk shads, Micro jigs / slow-pitch jigs, Vibes.

When is the best time to catch Snapper?

Snapper are at their best in May–Sep (good from Jan–Dec). They bite best dawn and dusk and around the tide change, in water around 15–22°C.

Where can you catch Snapper?

Inshore and offshore reefs, gravel beds, washy rocky headlands. Top spots on CastCall include Kurnell, Sydney Offshore Reefs, Norah Head Rocks, Port Stephens, Broughton Island, South West Rocks.

What is the legal size for Snapper?

NSW: min 30 cm, bag 10 (guide only). Size and bag limits differ between states and change over time, so always check the current rules before keeping fish.
