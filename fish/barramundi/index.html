Barramundi fishing: best bait, lures, rigs & where to catch them

Lates calcarifer · Tidal rivers, creek mouths, mangrove snags, rock bars and floodplain run-offs across northern Australia. Fish the run-out tide where creeks and drains empty into the main river. ⚠️ Crocodile country – stay well back from the edge and never clean fish at the water.

Best baits for Barramundi

  • Live mullet
  • Live prawns
  • Live cherabin (freshwater prawns)

Best lures

  • Shallow-diving hardbodies (gold and natural colours)
  • 4–6" paddle-tail soft plastics
  • Soft vibes worked along drop-offs

Rig

30–50lb braid, 40–60lb leader, 4/0–6/0 hooks for live bait. Cast lures tight to snags and roll them slowly.

Size and bag limit

Check current NSW DPI rules. Limits differ between states – always check your state's current rules.

Common questions

What is the best bait for Barramundi?

The best baits for Barramundi are Live mullet, Live prawns, Live cherabin (freshwater prawns). Lures that work: Shallow-diving hardbodies (gold and natural colours), 4–6" paddle-tail soft plastics, Soft vibes worked along drop-offs.

When is the best time to catch Barramundi?

Barramundi are at their best in Oct–Mar (good from Sep–May). They bite best dawn and dusk and around the tide change, in water around 24–32°C.

Where can you catch Barramundi?

Tidal rivers, creek mouths, mangrove snags, rock bars and floodplain run-offs across northern Australia. Top spots on CastCall include Townsville, Cairns, Lake Tinaroo, Darwin Harbour, Mary River, Daly River.

What is the legal size for Barramundi?

Check current NSW DPI rules. Size and bag limits differ between states and change over time, so always check the current rules before keeping fish.
