Common questions

What is the best bait for WA Dhufish?

The best baits for WA Dhufish are Octopus, Squid, Fresh mulie (pilchard) or fish fillet. Lures that work: Heavy soft plastics, Slow-pitch jigs.

When is the best time to catch WA Dhufish?

WA Dhufish are at their best in all year (good from Dec–Sep). They bite best dawn and during the day and on any tide, in water around 16–23°C.

Where can you catch WA Dhufish?

Offshore limestone reef and lumps off WA's west coast, often 20–60 m deep. Top spots on CastCall include Rottnest Island.

What is the legal size for WA Dhufish?

Check current NSW DPI rules. Size and bag limits differ between states and change over time, so always check the current rules before keeping fish.
