Common questions

What is the best bait for European Carp?

The best baits for European Carp are Sweet corn, Bread / dough balls, Scrub worms. Lures that work: Not a lure fish.

When is the best time to catch European Carp?

European Carp are at their best in Oct–Mar (good from Sep–Apr). They bite best dawn and dusk and on any tide, in water around 15–28°C.

Where can you catch European Carp?

Almost every river and dam – muddy bays, shallow flats and slow water. Top spots on CastCall include Homebush Bay, Nepean River, Lake Lyell, Lake Burrendong, Lake St Clair, Glenbawn Dam.

What is the legal size for European Carp?

Pest – no size or bag limit, don’t release alive. Size and bag limits differ between states and change over time, so always check the current rules before keeping fish.
