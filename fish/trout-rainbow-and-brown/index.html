Best baits for Trout

  • Scrub worms
  • Mudeyes under a float
  • PowerBait dough
  • Wood grubs

Best lures

  • Tassie Devils (trolled or cast)
  • Small inline spinners
  • Small soft plastics / minnows
  • Flies: nymphs, woolly buggers, dry flies

Rig

4–6lb line. Float rig with a mudeye, or a running sinker with a worm off the bottom. Troll Tassie Devils 1–2 km/h.

Size and bag limit

Check current NSW DPI rules. Limits differ between states – always check your state's current rules.

Common questions

What is the best bait for Trout?

The best baits for Trout are Scrub worms, Mudeyes under a float, PowerBait dough, Wood grubs. Lures that work: Tassie Devils (trolled or cast), Small inline spinners, Small soft plastics / minnows, Flies: nymphs, woolly buggers, dry flies.

When is the best time to catch Trout?

Trout are at their best in Mar–May, Oct–Nov (good from Jan–Dec). They bite best dawn and dusk and on any tide, in water around 8–18°C.

Where can you catch Trout?

Cold Snowy Mountains and tableland lakes and streams – drop-offs, weed edges and river inflows. Top spots on CastCall include Lake Lyell, Lake Eucumbene, Lake Jindabyne, Thredbo River, Burrinjuck Dam, Lake Eildon.

What is the legal size for Trout?

Check current NSW DPI rules. Size and bag limits differ between states and change over time, so always check the current rules before keeping fish.
