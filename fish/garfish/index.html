Common questions

What is the best bait for Garfish?

The best baits for Garfish are Bread, Maggots, Tiny pieces of prawn, Silverfish. Lures that work: Not a lure fish.

When is the best time to catch Garfish?

Garfish are at their best in Mar–May (good from Jan–Dec). They bite best dawn and on any tide, in water around 14–24°C.

Where can you catch Garfish?

Calm bays, jetties and weed beds just under the surface. Top spots on CastCall include Mandurah Estuary, Busselton Jetty, Esperance, Adelaide Jetties, Kangaroo Island, Yorke Peninsula.

What is the legal size for Garfish?

Check current NSW DPI rules. Size and bag limits differ between states and change over time, so always check the current rules before keeping fish.
