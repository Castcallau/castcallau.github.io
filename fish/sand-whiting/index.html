Sand Whiting fishing: best bait, lures, rigs & where to catch them

Sillago ciliata · Shallow sand flats, beach gutters and channel edges. Wade or drift the flats on the rising tide. Keep baits moving slowly – whiting love a slowly retrieved worm.

Best baits for Sand Whiting

  • Beach worms
  • Bloodworms
  • Live nippers (yabbies)
  • Pipis
  • Peeled prawn

Best lures

  • Small surface poppers / stickbaits (summer, hot flats)
  • Tiny worm-imitation soft plastics

Rig

Long-leader running sinker rig: small ball sinker, 1 m of 6lb leader, #4–#6 long-shank hook, a couple of red beads above the hook.

Size and bag limit

NSW: min 27 cm, bag 20 (guide only). Limits differ between states – always check your state's current rules.

Common questions

What is the best bait for Sand Whiting?

The best baits for Sand Whiting are Beach worms, Bloodworms, Live nippers (yabbies), Pipis, Peeled prawn. Lures that work: Small surface poppers / stickbaits (summer, hot flats), Tiny worm-imitation soft plastics.

When is the best time to catch Sand Whiting?

Sand Whiting are at their best in Nov–Mar (good from Oct–Apr). They bite best during the day and dusk and on the rising (run-in) tide, in water around 19–28°C.

Where can you catch Sand Whiting?

Shallow sand flats, beach gutters and channel edges. Top spots on CastCall include Kurnell, Port Hacking, Maroubra Beach, Georges River, Narrabeen Lake Entrance, Pittwater.

What is the legal size for Sand Whiting?

NSW: min 27 cm, bag 20 (guide only). Size and bag limits differ between states and change over time, so always check the current rules before keeping fish.
