Best baits for Mahi Mahi

  • Live yakkas / slimies

Best lures

  • Skirted trolling lures
  • 4–6" soft plastics
  • Small poppers

Rig

Livebait on a 4/0–6/0 circle hook, 30–40lb leader. Keep one hooked fish in the water and the school stays.

Size and bag limit

NSW: min 50 cm, bag 10 (guide only). Limits differ between states – always check your state's current rules.

Common questions

What is the best bait for Mahi Mahi?

The best baits for Mahi Mahi are Live yakkas / slimies, Pilchards or squid cubes. Lures that work: Skirted trolling lures, 4–6" soft plastics, Small poppers.

When is the best time to catch Mahi Mahi?

Mahi Mahi are at their best in Jan–Mar (good from Dec–Apr). They bite best dawn and during the day and on any tide, in water around 22–29°C.

Where can you catch Mahi Mahi?

Offshore FADs, floating debris and warm East Australian Current eddies. Top spots on CastCall include Sydney Offshore Reefs, Broughton Island, Solitary Islands, Great Barrier Reef, Exmouth.

What is the legal size for Mahi Mahi?

NSW: min 50 cm, bag 10 (guide only). Size and bag limits differ between states and change over time, so always check the current rules before keeping fish.
