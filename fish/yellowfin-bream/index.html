Best baits for Yellowfin Bream

  • Peeled prawn
  • Live nippers (yabbies)
  • Mullet / tuna strip
  • Pilchard pieces
  • Bread (berley with it)

Best lures

  • 2–3" soft plastic grubs on 1/16–1/8oz jighead
  • Small deep-diving hardbody crankbaits
  • Surface poppers / walkers in summer

Rig

Running ball sinker (as light as possible) to a #1–1/0 hook on 6–8lb fluorocarbon. Unweighted baits drifted in a berley trail work best around structure.

Size and bag limit

NSW: min 25 cm, bag 20 (combined bream) (guide only). Limits differ between states – always check your state's current rules.

Common questions

What is the best bait for Yellowfin Bream?

The best baits for Yellowfin Bream are Peeled prawn, Live nippers (yabbies), Mullet / tuna strip, Pilchard pieces, Bread (berley with it). Lures that work: 2–3" soft plastic grubs on 1/16–1/8oz jighead, Small deep-diving hardbody crankbaits, Surface poppers / walkers in summer.

When is the best time to catch Yellowfin Bream?

Yellowfin Bream are at their best in May–Aug (good from Jan–Dec). They bite best dawn and dusk and on the rising (run-in) tide, in water around 14–26°C.

Where can you catch Yellowfin Bream?

Everywhere – oyster racks, wharves, rock walls, snags, washes. Top spots on CastCall include Kurnell, Clifton Gardens Wharf, Middle Harbour, Port Hacking, Hawkesbury River, Long Reef Headland.

What is the legal size for Yellowfin Bream?

NSW: min 25 cm, bag 20 (combined bream) (guide only). Size and bag limits differ between states and change over time, so always check the current rules before keeping fish.
