Best lures

  • Deep-diving hardbodies trolled or cast
  • Spinnerbaits
  • Lipless crankbaits / blades hopped near timber

Rig

Running ball sinker down to a #1–2/0 hook, 10–15lb line. Fish baits right on the bottom among timber.

Size and bag limit

NSW: min 30 cm, bag 5 (guide only). Limits differ between states – always check your state's current rules.

Common questions

What is the best bait for Golden Perch?

The best baits for Golden Perch are Live yabbies, Live shrimp, Scrub worms, Bardi grubs. Lures that work: Deep-diving hardbodies trolled or cast, Spinnerbaits, Lipless crankbaits / blades hopped near timber.

When is the best time to catch Golden Perch?

Golden Perch are at their best in Oct–Mar (good from Sep–Apr). They bite best dawn and dusk and on any tide, in water around 18–28°C.

Where can you catch Golden Perch?

Inland dams and rivers – drowned timber, rocky points and creek mouths. Top spots on CastCall include Lake Burrendong, Windamere Dam, Lake St Clair, Glenbawn Dam, Lake Keepit, Copeton Dam.

What is the legal size for Golden Perch?

NSW: min 30 cm, bag 5 (guide only). Size and bag limits differ between states and change over time, so always check the current rules before keeping fish.
