Australian fish guide: best bait, lures and seasons

How to catch 33 of Australia's most popular fish – the best bait and lures, rigs, best months and where to find them.

Saltwater fish

Yellowfin Bream

Best months: May–Aug · Top baits: Peeled prawn, Live nippers (yabbies), Mullet / tuna strip

Dusky Flathead

Best months: Oct–Mar · Top baits: Live poddy mullet, Whole prawn, Pilchard / whitebait

Sand Whiting

Best months: Nov–Mar · Top baits: Beach worms, Bloodworms, Live nippers (yabbies)

Tailor

Best months: Apr–Jul · Top baits: Pilchard on gang hooks, Garfish, Fresh bonito / mullet strip

Australian Salmon

Best months: Apr–Aug · Top baits: Pilchard on gang hooks, Beach worms, Peeled prawn

Mulloway (Jewfish)

Best months: Nov–Apr · Top baits: Live mullet / yellowtail / squid, Fresh squid strips or heads, Fresh tailor / bonito fillet

Luderick (Blackfish)

Best months: Apr–Aug · Top baits: Green weed (tidal ribbon weed), Cabbage weed (on the rocks), Squirt worms

Yellowtail Kingfish

Best months: Nov–Apr · Top baits: Live squid, Live yellowtail (yakkas) or slimy mackerel, Strip baits on a downrigger

Snapper

Best months: May–Sep · Top baits: Pilchard (unweighted floater), Squid or cuttlefish strips, Fresh yakka / slimy mackerel fillet

Squid (Southern Calamari)

Best months: May–Oct · Top baits: Pilchard / mullet on a squid spike, Live yellowtail on a squid jag

Silver Trevally

Best months: May–Aug · Top baits: Peeled prawn, Pilchard pieces, Squid strips

Black Drummer (Pig)

Best months: Apr–Sep · Top baits: Cabbage weed, Bread, Peeled prawn

Mahi Mahi (Dolphinfish)

Best months: Jan–Mar · Top baits: Live yakkas / slimies, Pilchards or squid cubes

Spotted & Spanish Mackerel

Best months: Feb–Apr · Top baits: Live slimy mackerel / yakka, Whole garfish or pilchard trolled slowly

Australian Bonito

Best months: Apr–Jun · Top baits: Whole pilchard, Live yakka

Tiger Flathead

Best months: all year · Top baits: Squid strips, Pilchard / mullet strips, Prawn

Barramundi

Best months: Oct–Mar · Top baits: Live mullet, Live prawns, Live cherabin (freshwater prawns)

Mangrove Jack

Best months: Nov–Mar · Top baits: Live mullet / herring, Live prawns, Fresh mullet or squid strips

Coral Trout

Best months: Apr–Oct · Top baits: Whole pilchard, Live fusilier / hardiheads, Squid

Threadfin Salmon

Best months: Oct–Mar · Top baits: Live prawns, Live mullet, Peeled prawn

Queenfish

Best months: Apr–Oct · Top baits: Live herring / mullet, Whole garfish

King George Whiting

Best months: Nov–Apr · Top baits: Pipis, Squid strips, Mussel

Australian Herring (Tommy Ruff)

Best months: Apr–Jun · Top baits: Maggots / wogs under a float, Prawn pieces, Squid strips

Black Bream

Best months: Sep–Nov · Top baits: Sandworm / bloodworm, Spider crab, Peeled prawn

WA Dhufish

Best months: all year · Top baits: Octopus, Squid, Fresh mulie (pilchard) or fish fillet

Sand Flathead

Best months: Oct–Apr · Top baits: Squid strips, Pilchard pieces, Prawn

Garfish

Best months: Mar–May · Top baits: Bread, Maggots, Tiny pieces of prawn

Freshwater fish

Australian Bass

Best months: Oct–Mar · Top baits: Live shrimp, Crickets / cicadas, Scrub worms

Golden Perch (Yellowbelly)

Best months: Oct–Mar · Top baits: Live yabbies, Live shrimp, Scrub worms

Murray Cod

Best months: Jan–Apr · Top baits: Live yabbies, Bardi grubs, Big scrub worm bunches

Trout (Rainbow & Brown)

Best months: Mar–May, Oct–Nov · Top baits: Scrub worms, Mudeyes under a float, PowerBait dough

Redfin (English Perch)

Best months: Mar–Apr, Oct–Nov · Top baits: Scrub worms, Small yabbies, Live shrimp

European Carp

Best months: Oct–Mar · Top baits: Sweet corn, Bread / dough balls, Scrub worms
