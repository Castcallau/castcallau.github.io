Common questions

What is the best bait for Threadfin Salmon?

The best baits for Threadfin Salmon are Live prawns, Live mullet, Peeled prawn. Lures that work: Soft vibes, Paddle-tail soft plastics hopped on the bottom.

When is the best time to catch Threadfin Salmon?

Threadfin Salmon are at their best in Oct–Mar (good from Jan–Dec). They bite best dawn and dusk and around the tide change, in water around 24–32°C.

Where can you catch Threadfin Salmon?

Muddy tidal rivers, creek mouths and mud flats in the tropics – often with barra. Top spots on CastCall include Townsville, Cairns, Darwin Harbour, Mary River, Broome.

What is the legal size for Threadfin Salmon?

Check current NSW DPI rules. Size and bag limits differ between states and change over time, so always check the current rules before keeping fish.
