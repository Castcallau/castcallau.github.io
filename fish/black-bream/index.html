Common questions

What is the best bait for Black Bream?

The best baits for Black Bream are Sandworm / bloodworm, Spider crab, Peeled prawn, Mussel, Pod / spinyhead worm. Lures that work: Small soft plastic grubs, Small deep-diving crankbaits, Surface walkers in summer.

When is the best time to catch Black Bream?

Black Bream are at their best in Sep–Nov (good from Jan–Dec). They bite best dawn and dusk and at night and on the rising (run-in) tide, in water around 12–24°C.

Where can you catch Black Bream?

Southern estuaries and rivers – snags, jetties, rock walls and weed edges (VIC, SA, WA, TAS). Top spots on CastCall include Swan River, Mandurah Estuary, Albany, Coorong & Murray Mouth, Gippsland Lakes, Mallacoota Inlet.

What is the legal size for Black Bream?

Check current NSW DPI rules. Size and bag limits differ between states and change over time, so always check the current rules before keeping fish.
