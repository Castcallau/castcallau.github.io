Common questions

What is the best bait for Tiger Flathead?

The best baits for Tiger Flathead are Squid strips, Pilchard / mullet strips, Prawn. Lures that work: Soft plastics on heavy jigheads bounced on a drift.

When is the best time to catch Tiger Flathead?

Tiger Flathead are at their best in all year (good from Jan–Dec). They bite best dawn and during the day and on any tide, in water around 13–21°C.

Where can you catch Tiger Flathead?

Offshore sand and mud grounds, roughly 30–80 m deep. Top spots on CastCall include Sydney Offshore Reefs, Broughton Island, Solitary Islands, Montague Island.

What is the legal size for Tiger Flathead?

Check current NSW DPI rules. Size and bag limits differ between states and change over time, so always check the current rules before keeping fish.
