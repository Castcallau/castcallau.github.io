Dusky Flathead fishing: best bait, lures, rigs & where to catch them

Platycephalus fuscus · Sand and mud flats, weed edges, drop-offs and channel edges in estuaries. Fish the last half of the run-out tide where bait drains off the flats. Drift or walk the edges – flathead ambush, so keep moving until you find them.

Best baits for Dusky Flathead

  • Live poddy mullet
  • Whole prawn
  • Pilchard / whitebait
  • Fresh mullet strip

Best lures

  • 3–5" paddle-tail soft plastics hopped on the bottom
  • Blade / vibe lures
  • Shallow hardbodies over flats

Rig

Running sinker down to a swivel, 60 cm of 15–20lb leader (they have sharp gill plates) and a 1/0–3/0 long-shank hook.

Size and bag limit

NSW: min 36 cm, bag 10 (only 1 over 70 cm) (guide only). Limits differ between states – always check your state's current rules.

Common questions

What is the best bait for Dusky Flathead?

The best baits for Dusky Flathead are Live poddy mullet, Whole prawn, Pilchard / whitebait, Fresh mullet strip. Lures that work: 3–5" paddle-tail soft plastics hopped on the bottom, Blade / vibe lures, Shallow hardbodies over flats.

When is the best time to catch Dusky Flathead?

Dusky Flathead are at their best in Oct–Mar (good from Sep–Apr). They bite best dawn and dusk and on the falling (run-out) tide, in water around 17–27°C.

Where can you catch Dusky Flathead?

Sand and mud flats, weed edges, drop-offs and channel edges in estuaries. Top spots on CastCall include Kurnell, Middle Harbour, Port Hacking, Hawkesbury River, Georges River, Narrabeen Lake Entrance.

What is the legal size for Dusky Flathead?

NSW: min 36 cm, bag 10 (only 1 over 70 cm) (guide only). Size and bag limits differ between states and change over time, so always check the current rules before keeping fish.
