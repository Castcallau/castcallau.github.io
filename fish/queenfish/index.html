Common questions

What is the best bait for Queenfish?

The best baits for Queenfish are Live herring / mullet, Whole garfish. Lures that work: Surface poppers and stickbaits, Metal slugs cranked fast, Soft plastic jerkbaits.

When is the best time to catch Queenfish?

Queenfish are at their best in Apr–Oct (good from Jan–Dec). They bite best dawn and dusk and around the tide change, in water around 24–31°C.

Where can you catch Queenfish?

River mouths, headlands, flats and current lines in northern waters. Top spots on CastCall include Hervey Bay, Whitsundays, Townsville, Cairns, Darwin Harbour, Dundee Beach.

What is the legal size for Queenfish?

Check current NSW DPI rules. Size and bag limits differ between states and change over time, so always check the current rules before keeping fish.
