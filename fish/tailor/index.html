Best baits for Tailor

  • Pilchard on gang hooks
  • Garfish
  • Fresh bonito / mullet strip

Best lures

  • 20–40g metal slugs (fast retrieve)
  • Surface poppers
  • Shallow minnows

Rig

Ganged 3–4 × 3/0–4/0 hooks through a whole pilchard, 15–20lb line, little or no sinker in the wash.

Size and bag limit

NSW: min 30 cm, bag 10 (guide only). Limits differ between states – always check your state's current rules.

Common questions

What is the best bait for Tailor?

The best baits for Tailor are Pilchard on gang hooks, Garfish, Fresh bonito / mullet strip. Lures that work: 20–40g metal slugs (fast retrieve), Surface poppers, Shallow minnows.

When is the best time to catch Tailor?

Tailor are at their best in Apr–Jul (good from Mar–Sep). They bite best dawn and dusk and on the rising (run-in) tide, in water around 16–23°C.

Where can you catch Tailor?

Beach gutters, rock washes, breakwall ends, harbour points. Top spots on CastCall include Clifton Gardens Wharf, Long Reef Headland, Maroubra Beach, The Entrance, Norah Head Rocks, Lake Macquarie.

What is the legal size for Tailor?

NSW: min 30 cm, bag 10 (guide only). Size and bag limits differ between states and change over time, so always check the current rules before keeping fish.
