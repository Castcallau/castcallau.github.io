Common questions

What is the best bait for Sand Flathead?

The best baits for Sand Flathead are Squid strips, Pilchard pieces, Prawn, Fish fillet. Lures that work: Soft plastics bounced along the bottom on a drift.

When is the best time to catch Sand Flathead?

Sand Flathead are at their best in Oct–Apr (good from Sep–Jun). They bite best dawn and during the day and on any tide, in water around 11–21°C.

Where can you catch Sand Flathead?

Sandy and muddy bottoms in bays, estuaries and inshore waters of VIC, TAS and SA. Top spots on CastCall include St Kilda Pier, Port Phillip Bay, Western Port, Derwent River, Eaglehawk Neck & Pirates Bay, St Helens.

What is the legal size for Sand Flathead?

Check current NSW DPI rules. Size and bag limits differ between states and change over time, so always check the current rules before keeping fish.
