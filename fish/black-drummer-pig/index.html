Rig

Heavy and short: 20–30lb line, small ball sinker right on top of a 1/0–2/0 suicide hook. Hold on – they bury you in the reef.

Common questions

What is the best bait for Black Drummer?

The best baits for Black Drummer are Cabbage weed, Bread, Peeled prawn, Cunjevoi. Lures that work: Bait only.

When is the best time to catch Black Drummer?

Black Drummer are at their best in Apr–Sep (good from Jan–Dec). They bite best dawn and on any tide, in water around 14–23°C.

Where can you catch Black Drummer?

Shallow rocky washes and gutters right at your feet on rock platforms. Top spots on CastCall include Long Reef Headland, Norah Head Rocks, Bass Point, Cronulla, Ben Buckler, Terrigal Haven.

What is the legal size for Black Drummer?

Check current NSW DPI rules. Size and bag limits differ between states and change over time, so always check the current rules before keeping fish.
