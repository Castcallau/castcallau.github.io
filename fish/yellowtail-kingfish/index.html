Best baits for Yellowtail Kingfish

  • Live squid
  • Live yellowtail (yakkas) or slimy mackerel
  • Strip baits on a downrigger

Best lures

  • Surface stickbaits / poppers
  • Metal jigs 60–200g
  • 6–7" soft plastic jerkbaits

Rig

Livebait on a 6/0–8/0 circle hook, 40–60lb leader. Lock the drag – they head straight for the reef.

Size and bag limit

NSW: min 65 cm, bag 5 (guide only). Limits differ between states – always check your state's current rules.

Common questions

What is the best bait for Yellowtail Kingfish?

The best baits for Yellowtail Kingfish are Live squid, Live yellowtail (yakkas) or slimy mackerel, Strip baits on a downrigger. Lures that work: Surface stickbaits / poppers, Metal jigs 60–200g, 6–7" soft plastic jerkbaits.

When is the best time to catch Yellowtail Kingfish?

Yellowtail Kingfish are at their best in Nov–Apr (good from Oct–May). They bite best dawn and around the tide change, in water around 17–24°C.

Where can you catch Yellowtail Kingfish?

Headlands, deep rock ledges, harbour markers, offshore reefs. Top spots on CastCall include Kurnell, Clifton Gardens Wharf, Middle Harbour, Hawkesbury River, Long Reef Headland, Sydney Offshore Reefs.

What is the legal size for Yellowtail Kingfish?

NSW: min 65 cm, bag 5 (guide only). Size and bag limits differ between states and change over time, so always check the current rules before keeping fish.
