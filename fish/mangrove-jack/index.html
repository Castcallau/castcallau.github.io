Best baits for Mangrove Jack

  • Live mullet / herring
  • Live prawns
  • Fresh mullet or squid strips

Best lures

  • Shallow hardbodies cast tight to snags
  • 3–4" soft plastic prawns and shads
  • Surface walkers at dawn and dusk

Rig

Heavy and short: 30–50lb leader, 3/0–5/0 hook, drag locked – they go straight back into the snag.

Common questions

What is the best bait for Mangrove Jack?

The best baits for Mangrove Jack are Live mullet / herring, Live prawns, Fresh mullet or squid strips. Lures that work: Shallow hardbodies cast tight to snags, 3–4" soft plastic prawns and shads, Surface walkers at dawn and dusk.

When is the best time to catch Mangrove Jack?

Mangrove Jack are at their best in Nov–Mar (good from Oct–Apr). They bite best dawn and dusk and at night and around the tide change, in water around 24–31°C.

Where can you catch Mangrove Jack?

Mangrove snags, rock walls, bridge pylons and rock bars in warm estuaries. Top spots on CastCall include Gold Coast Seaway, Redcliffe Jetty & Scarborough, Noosa River & Heads, Whitsundays, Townsville, Cairns.

What is the legal size for Mangrove Jack?

Check current NSW DPI rules. Size and bag limits differ between states and change over time, so always check the current rules before keeping fish.
