Mulloway fishing: best bait, lures, rigs & where to catch them

Argyrosomus japonicus · Deep holes in rivers, bridge pylons, breakwalls, beach gutters at night. Tide changes after dark around new and full moons are prime. Discoloured water after rain fires them up in the rivers.

Best baits for Mulloway

  • Live mullet / yellowtail / squid
  • Fresh squid strips or heads
  • Fresh tailor / bonito fillet
  • Whole pilchard (fresh, not stale)

Best lures

  • 5–7" soft plastics on 3/8–1oz jigheads
  • Large vibes worked slowly at night

Rig

Running sinker or snelled two-hook rig (6/0–8/0) on 40–60lb leader. Leave the bail arm open or drag light for the run.

Size and bag limit

NSW: min 70 cm, bag 2 (guide only). Limits differ between states – always check your state's current rules.

Common questions

What is the best bait for Mulloway?

The best baits for Mulloway are Live mullet / yellowtail / squid, Fresh squid strips or heads, Fresh tailor / bonito fillet, Whole pilchard (fresh, not stale). Lures that work: 5–7" soft plastics on 3/8–1oz jigheads, Large vibes worked slowly at night.

When is the best time to catch Mulloway?

Mulloway are at their best in Nov–Apr (good from Sep–Jun). They bite best dusk and at night and around the tide change, in water around 17–26°C.

Where can you catch Mulloway?

Deep holes in rivers, bridge pylons, breakwalls, beach gutters at night. Top spots on CastCall include Middle Harbour, Hawkesbury River, Maroubra Beach, Georges River, The Entrance, Lake Macquarie.

What is the legal size for Mulloway?

NSW: min 70 cm, bag 2 (guide only). Size and bag limits differ between states and change over time, so always check the current rules before keeping fish.
