Best baits for Murray Cod

  • Live yabbies
  • Bardi grubs
  • Big scrub worm bunches
  • Cheese / chicken (common local baits)

Best lures

  • Large spinnerbaits
  • Big deep-diving hardbodies
  • Surface wake baits at night in summer
  • Swimbaits

Rig

Heavy gear: 30–50lb braid, 40–60lb leader, running sinker to a 4/0–8/0 hook or lures slow-rolled past snags.

Size and bag limit

NSW: 55–75 cm (slot), bag 2 (guide only). Limits differ between states – always check your state's current rules.

Common questions

What is the best bait for Murray Cod?

The best baits for Murray Cod are Live yabbies, Bardi grubs, Big scrub worm bunches, Cheese / chicken (common local baits). Lures that work: Large spinnerbaits, Big deep-diving hardbodies, Surface wake baits at night in summer, Swimbaits.

When is the best time to catch Murray Cod?

Murray Cod are at their best in Jan–Apr (good from Dec–May). They bite best dawn and dusk and at night and on any tide, in water around 14–26°C.

Where can you catch Murray Cod?

Big snags, undercut banks and deep holes in the Murray–Darling rivers and inland dams. Top spots on CastCall include Lake Burrendong, Windamere Dam, Glenbawn Dam, Lake Keepit, Copeton Dam, Burrinjuck Dam.

What is the legal size for Murray Cod?

NSW: 55–75 cm (slot), bag 2 (guide only). Size and bag limits differ between states and change over time, so always check the current rules before keeping fish.
