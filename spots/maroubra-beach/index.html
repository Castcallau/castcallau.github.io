Maroubra Beach fishing

A surf beach in Sydney, New South Wales – land-based. Deep gutters form along the beach. Salmon and tailor in winter, whiting in summer, the odd mulloway at night.

Depth & structure

Within about 450 m of Maroubra Beach the seabed reaches about 14 m deep, with a drop-off from about 3 m to 9 m – drop-offs and holes like this are where fish hold, so fish the edges. Open the live forecast for the full depth map, deep holes and reefs (NSW Government 2018 survey).

Getting there

Plenty of parking on Marine Pde. Avoid the flagged swim area.

Common questions

What fish can you catch at Maroubra Beach?

Maroubra Beach holds Australian Salmon, Tailor, Sand Whiting, Yellowfin Bream and Mulloway. Deep gutters form along the beach. Salmon and tailor in winter, whiting in summer, the odd mulloway at night.

What is the best bait at Maroubra Beach?

Good baits here are Pilchard on gang hooks, Beach worms, Garfish, Bloodworms, Peeled prawn, Live nippers (yabbies). For Australian Salmon, try Pilchard on gang hooks, Beach worms, Peeled prawn.

When is the best time to fish Maroubra Beach?

The best months are usually Mar–Jun. On the day, dawn and dusk and the hours around the tide change are typically best. CastCall scores Maroubra Beach every hour for the next two weeks using tides, wind, swell, water temperature and the moon.
