Hervey Bay – Urangan Pier fishing

A harbour and bay in Fraser Coast, Queensland – land-based and boat / kayak. The 870 m Urangan Pier reaches deep water – mackerel and queenies in summer, whiting and bream all year.

Common questions

What fish can you catch at Hervey Bay?

Hervey Bay holds Sand Whiting, Yellowfin Bream, Dusky Flathead, Spotted & Spanish Mackerel, Queenfish, Silver Trevally and Squid. The 870 m Urangan Pier reaches deep water – mackerel and queenies in summer, whiting and bream all year.

What is the best bait at Hervey Bay?

Good baits here are Beach worms, Bloodworms, Peeled prawn, Live nippers (yabbies), Live poddy mullet, Whole prawn. For Sand Whiting, try Beach worms, Bloodworms, Live nippers (yabbies).

When is the best time to fish Hervey Bay?

The best months are usually Feb–May. On the day, dawn and dusk and the hours around the tide change are typically best. CastCall scores Hervey Bay every hour for the next two weeks using tides, wind, swell, water temperature and the moon.
