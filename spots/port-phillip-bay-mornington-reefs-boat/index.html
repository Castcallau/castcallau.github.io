Common questions

What fish can you catch at Port Phillip Bay?

Port Phillip Bay holds Snapper, King George Whiting, Squid, Sand Flathead and Yellowtail Kingfish. The spring snapper run on the inshore reefs is Melbourne's favourite. Kingfish near the Heads in summer.

What is the best bait at Port Phillip Bay?

Good baits here are Pilchard (unweighted floater), Squid or cuttlefish strips, Pipis, Squid strips, Pilchard / mullet on a squid spike, Live yellowtail on a squid jag. For Snapper, try Pilchard (unweighted floater), Squid or cuttlefish strips, Fresh yakka / slimy mackerel fillet.

When is the best time to fish Port Phillip Bay?

The best months are usually Nov–Apr. On the day, dawn and dusk and the hours around the tide change are typically best. CastCall scores Port Phillip Bay every hour for the next two weeks using tides, wind, swell, water temperature and the moon.
