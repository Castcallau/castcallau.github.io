Common questions

What fish can you catch at Portland?

Portland holds Yellowtail Kingfish, Snapper, Squid, Australian Salmon and King George Whiting. Kingfish off the breakwall in summer, snapper in the bay, and squid all year.

What is the best bait at Portland?

Good baits here are Live squid, Live yellowtail (yakkas) or slimy mackerel, Pilchard (unweighted floater), Squid or cuttlefish strips, Pilchard / mullet on a squid spike, Live yellowtail on a squid jag. For Yellowtail Kingfish, try Live squid, Live yellowtail (yakkas) or slimy mackerel, Strip baits on a downrigger.

When is the best time to fish Portland?

The best months are usually Mar–Jun. On the day, dawn and dusk and the hours around the tide change are typically best. CastCall scores Portland every hour for the next two weeks using tides, wind, swell, water temperature and the moon.
