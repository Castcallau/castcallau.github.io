Bundeena Wharf fishing

An estuary in Sydney, New South Wales – land-based. Ferry wharf on the south side of Port Hacking. Squid and trevally off the wharf, whiting on the sand flats nearby.

Depth & structure

Within about 450 m of Bundeena Wharf the seabed reaches about 5 m deep, with a drop-off from about 1 m to 4 m – drop-offs and holes like this are where fish hold, so fish the edges. Open the live forecast for the full depth map, deep holes and reefs (NSW Government 2018 survey).

Getting there

Ferry from Cronulla or drive through Royal National Park. Keep clear of the ferry.

Common questions

What fish can you catch at Bundeena Wharf?

Bundeena Wharf holds Squid, Yellowfin Bream, Silver Trevally, Sand Whiting, Dusky Flathead and Tailor. Ferry wharf on the south side of Port Hacking. Squid and trevally off the wharf, whiting on the sand flats nearby.

What is the best bait at Bundeena Wharf?

Good baits here are Pilchard / mullet on a squid spike, Live yellowtail on a squid jag, Peeled prawn, Live nippers (yabbies), Pilchard pieces, Beach worms. For Squid, try Pilchard / mullet on a squid spike, Live yellowtail on a squid jag.

When is the best time to fish Bundeena Wharf?

The best months are usually Mar–Jul, Oct. On the day, dawn and dusk and the hours around the tide change are typically best. CastCall scores Bundeena Wharf every hour for the next two weeks using tides, wind, swell, water temperature and the moon.
