Common questions

What fish can you catch at Yorke Peninsula?

Yorke Peninsula holds Squid, King George Whiting, Garfish, Australian Herring and Snapper. Classic SA jetty with squid, garfish and tommy ruff under the lights.

What is the best bait at Yorke Peninsula?

Good baits here are Pilchard / mullet on a squid spike, Live yellowtail on a squid jag, Pipis, Squid strips, Bread, Maggots. For Squid, try Pilchard / mullet on a squid spike, Live yellowtail on a squid jag.

When is the best time to fish Yorke Peninsula?

The best months are usually Mar–Jun. On the day, dawn and dusk and the hours around the tide change are typically best. CastCall scores Yorke Peninsula every hour for the next two weeks using tides, wind, swell, water temperature and the moon.
