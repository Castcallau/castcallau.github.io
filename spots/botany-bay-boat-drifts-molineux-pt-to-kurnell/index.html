Botany Bay – Boat Drifts (Molineux Pt to Kurnell) fishing

A harbour and bay in Sydney, New South Wales – boat / kayak. Drift the edges of the dredged shipping channel and the drop-offs off Kurnell for flathead. Snapper and trevally on the deeper rubble, squid over the weed.

Depth & structure

Within about 450 m of Botany Bay the seabed reaches about 15 m deep, with a drop-off from about 10 m to 15 m – drop-offs and holes like this are where fish hold, so fish the edges. Open the live forecast for the full depth map, deep holes and reefs (NSW Government 2018 survey).

Getting there

Ramps at Foreshore Beach, Kurnell and Dolls Point. Keep clear of the shipping channel and port areas.

Common questions

What fish can you catch at Botany Bay?

Botany Bay holds Dusky Flathead, Snapper, Yellowfin Bream, Squid, Silver Trevally and Yellowtail Kingfish. Drift the edges of the dredged shipping channel and the drop-offs off Kurnell for flathead. Snapper and trevally on the deeper rubble, squid over the weed.

What is the best bait at Botany Bay?

Good baits here are Live poddy mullet, Whole prawn, Pilchard (unweighted floater), Squid or cuttlefish strips, Peeled prawn, Live nippers (yabbies). For Dusky Flathead, try Live poddy mullet, Whole prawn, Pilchard / whitebait.

When is the best time to fish Botany Bay?

The best months are usually Mar, May–Aug, Oct. On the day, dawn and dusk and the hours around the tide change are typically best. CastCall scores Botany Bay every hour for the next two weeks using tides, wind, swell, water temperature and the moon.
