Common questions

What fish can you catch at Burrinjuck Dam?

Burrinjuck Dam holds Murray Cod, Golden Perch, Redfin, Trout and European Carp. Deep, rugged dam with cod and yellowbelly in summer and trout in the cooler upper reaches.

What is the best bait at Burrinjuck Dam?

Good baits here are Live yabbies, Bardi grubs, Live shrimp, Scrub worms, Small yabbies, Mudeyes under a float. For Murray Cod, try Live yabbies, Bardi grubs, Big scrub worm bunches.

When is the best time to fish Burrinjuck Dam?

The best months are usually Jan–Apr. On the day, dawn and dusk and the hours around the tide change are typically best. CastCall scores Burrinjuck Dam every hour for the next two weeks using tides, wind, swell, water temperature and the moon.
