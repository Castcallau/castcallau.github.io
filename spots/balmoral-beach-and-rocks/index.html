Depth & structure

Within about 450 m of Balmoral Beach & Rocks the seabed reaches about 7 m deep, with a drop-off from about 1 m to 6 m – drop-offs and holes like this are where fish hold, so fish the edges. Open the live forecast for the full depth map, deep holes and reefs (NSW Government 2018 survey).

Getting there

Paid parking. Fish the ends of the beach away from swimmers.

Common questions

What fish can you catch at Balmoral Beach & Rocks?

Balmoral Beach & Rocks holds Squid, Yellowfin Bream, Silver Trevally, Dusky Flathead and Tailor. Weed beds off the beach are good for squid. Bream and trevally off the rocks at either end.

What is the best bait at Balmoral Beach & Rocks?

Good baits here are Pilchard / mullet on a squid spike, Live yellowtail on a squid jag, Peeled prawn, Live nippers (yabbies), Pilchard pieces, Live poddy mullet. For Squid, try Pilchard / mullet on a squid spike, Live yellowtail on a squid jag.

When is the best time to fish Balmoral Beach & Rocks?

The best months are usually May–Aug. On the day, dawn and dusk and the hours around the tide change are typically best. CastCall scores Balmoral Beach & Rocks every hour for the next two weeks using tides, wind, swell, water temperature and the moon.
