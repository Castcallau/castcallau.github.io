Batemans Bay – Clyde River fishing

An estuary in Illawarra & South Coast, New South Wales – boat / kayak and land-based. Big estuary system. Flathead on the flats, bream around the oyster leases, mulloway in the deeper river bends.

Depth & structure

Within about 450 m of Batemans Bay the seabed reaches about 7 m deep, with a drop-off from about 0 m to 7 m – drop-offs and holes like this are where fish hold, so fish the edges. Open the live forecast for the full depth map, deep holes and reefs (NSW Government 2018 survey).

Getting there

Ramps around town. Land-based from the bridge area and wharves.

Common questions

What fish can you catch at Batemans Bay?

Batemans Bay holds Dusky Flathead, Yellowfin Bream, Mulloway, Sand Whiting and Luderick. Big estuary system. Flathead on the flats, bream around the oyster leases, mulloway in the deeper river bends.

What is the best bait at Batemans Bay?

Good baits here are Live poddy mullet, Whole prawn, Peeled prawn, Live nippers (yabbies), Live mullet / yellowtail / squid, Fresh squid strips or heads. For Dusky Flathead, try Live poddy mullet, Whole prawn, Pilchard / whitebait.

When is the best time to fish Batemans Bay?

The best months are usually Nov–Apr. On the day, dawn and dusk and the hours around the tide change are typically best. CastCall scores Batemans Bay every hour for the next two weeks using tides, wind, swell, water temperature and the moon.
