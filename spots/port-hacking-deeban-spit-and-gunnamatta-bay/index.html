Port Hacking – Deeban Spit & Gunnamatta Bay fishing

An estuary in Sydney, New South Wales – land-based and boat / kayak. Huge sand flats at Deeban Spit – prime whiting and flathead on the run-in tide. Weed beds hold squid and luderick.

Depth & structure

Within about 450 m of Port Hacking the seabed reaches about 5 m deep, with a drop-off from about 0 m to 3 m – drop-offs and holes like this are where fish hold, so fish the edges. Open the live forecast for the full depth map, deep holes and reefs (NSW Government 2018 survey).

Getting there

Parking at Maianbar / Bonnie Vale (Royal National Park entry fee) or Gunnamatta Park, Cronulla.

Common questions

What fish can you catch at Port Hacking?

Port Hacking holds Sand Whiting, Dusky Flathead, Yellowfin Bream, Squid and Luderick. Huge sand flats at Deeban Spit – prime whiting and flathead on the run-in tide. Weed beds hold squid and luderick.

What is the best bait at Port Hacking?

Good baits here are Beach worms, Bloodworms, Live poddy mullet, Whole prawn, Peeled prawn, Live nippers (yabbies). For Sand Whiting, try Beach worms, Bloodworms, Live nippers (yabbies).

When is the best time to fish Port Hacking?

The best months are usually Oct–May. On the day, dawn and dusk and the hours around the tide change are typically best. CastCall scores Port Hacking every hour for the next two weeks using tides, wind, swell, water temperature and the moon.
