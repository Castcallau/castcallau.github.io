Mary River fishing

An estuary in Top End, Northern Territory – boat / kayak. One of Australia's great barra rivers. The run-off after the wet season is the famous bite.

Common questions

What fish can you catch at Mary River?

Mary River holds Barramundi and Threadfin Salmon. One of Australia's great barra rivers. The run-off after the wet season is the famous bite.

What is the best bait at Mary River?

Good baits here are Live mullet, Live prawns. For Barramundi, try Live mullet, Live prawns, Live cherabin (freshwater prawns).

When is the best time to fish Mary River?

The best months are usually Oct–Mar. On the day, dawn and dusk and the hours around the tide change are typically best. CastCall scores Mary River every hour for the next two weeks using tides, wind, swell, water temperature and the moon.
