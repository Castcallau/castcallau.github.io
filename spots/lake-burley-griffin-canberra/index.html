Common questions

What fish can you catch at Lake Burley Griffin?

Lake Burley Griffin holds Murray Cod, Golden Perch, Redfin and European Carp. City lake stocked with cod and yellowbelly. Redfin and carp along the shore.

What is the best bait at Lake Burley Griffin?

Good baits here are Live yabbies, Bardi grubs, Live shrimp, Scrub worms, Small yabbies, Sweet corn. For Murray Cod, try Live yabbies, Bardi grubs, Big scrub worm bunches.

When is the best time to fish Lake Burley Griffin?

The best months are usually Dec–Apr. On the day, dawn and dusk and the hours around the tide change are typically best. CastCall scores Lake Burley Griffin every hour for the next two weeks using tides, wind, swell, water temperature and the moon.
