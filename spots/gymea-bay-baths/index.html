Gymea Bay Baths fishing

An estuary in Sydney, New South Wales – land-based. Quiet little baths in a sheltered Port Hacking bay. Bream and trevally around the baths netting and jetty, squid over the weed at dusk, flathead and whiting on the sand.

Common questions

What fish can you catch at Gymea Bay Baths?

Gymea Bay Baths holds Yellowfin Bream, Dusky Flathead, Sand Whiting, Squid, Luderick and Silver Trevally. Quiet little baths in a sheltered Port Hacking bay. Bream and trevally around the baths netting and jetty, squid over the weed at dusk, flathead and whiting on the sand.

What is the best bait at Gymea Bay Baths?

Good baits here are Peeled prawn, Live nippers (yabbies), Live poddy mullet, Whole prawn, Beach worms, Bloodworms. For Yellowfin Bream, try Peeled prawn, Live nippers (yabbies), Mullet / tuna strip.

When is the best time to fish Gymea Bay Baths?

The best months are usually Mar–Aug, Oct. On the day, dawn and dusk and the hours around the tide change are typically best. CastCall scores Gymea Bay Baths every hour for the next two weeks using tides, wind, swell, water temperature and the moon.
