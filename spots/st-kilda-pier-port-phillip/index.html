Common questions

What fish can you catch at St Kilda Pier?

St Kilda Pier holds Snapper, Squid, King George Whiting, Sand Flathead, Australian Salmon and Garfish. City pier with snapper in spring, squid over the weed, salmon and flathead most of the year.

What is the best bait at St Kilda Pier?

Good baits here are Pilchard (unweighted floater), Squid or cuttlefish strips, Pilchard / mullet on a squid spike, Live yellowtail on a squid jag, Pipis, Squid strips. For Snapper, try Pilchard (unweighted floater), Squid or cuttlefish strips, Fresh yakka / slimy mackerel fillet.

When is the best time to fish St Kilda Pier?

The best months are usually Mar–Jun. On the day, dawn and dusk and the hours around the tide change are typically best. CastCall scores St Kilda Pier every hour for the next two weeks using tides, wind, swell, water temperature and the moon.
