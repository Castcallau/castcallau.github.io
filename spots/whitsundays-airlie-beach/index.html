Whitsundays – Airlie Beach fishing

A harbour and bay in Whitsundays, Queensland – boat / kayak and land-based. Island fringing reefs for coral trout, pelagics around the headlands and jacks in the creeks.

Common questions

What fish can you catch at Whitsundays?

Whitsundays holds Coral Trout, Queenfish, Spotted & Spanish Mackerel, Silver Trevally and Mangrove Jack. Island fringing reefs for coral trout, pelagics around the headlands and jacks in the creeks.

What is the best bait at Whitsundays?

Good baits here are Whole pilchard, Live fusilier / hardiheads, Live herring / mullet, Whole garfish, Live slimy mackerel / yakka, Whole garfish or pilchard trolled slowly. For Coral Trout, try Whole pilchard, Live fusilier / hardiheads, Squid.

When is the best time to fish Whitsundays?

The best months are usually Feb–May. On the day, dawn and dusk and the hours around the tide change are typically best. CastCall scores Whitsundays every hour for the next two weeks using tides, wind, swell, water temperature and the moon.
