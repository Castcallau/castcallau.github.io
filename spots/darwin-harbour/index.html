Darwin Harbour fishing

A harbour and bay in Top End, Northern Territory – boat / kayak and land-based. Huge tides and mangrove creeks. Barra and threadfin in the arms, queenies and mackerel on the harbour reefs.

Common questions

What fish can you catch at Darwin Harbour?

Darwin Harbour holds Barramundi, Threadfin Salmon, Queenfish, Mangrove Jack, Silver Trevally and Spotted & Spanish Mackerel. Huge tides and mangrove creeks. Barra and threadfin in the arms, queenies and mackerel on the harbour reefs.

What is the best bait at Darwin Harbour?

Good baits here are Live mullet, Live prawns, Live herring / mullet, Whole garfish, Live mullet / herring. For Barramundi, try Live mullet, Live prawns, Live cherabin (freshwater prawns).

When is the best time to fish Darwin Harbour?

The best months are usually Jan–Apr. On the day, dawn and dusk and the hours around the tide change are typically best. CastCall scores Darwin Harbour every hour for the next two weeks using tides, wind, swell, water temperature and the moon.
