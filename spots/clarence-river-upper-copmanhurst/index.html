Common questions

What fish can you catch at Clarence River?

Clarence River holds Australian Bass and European Carp. Wild river bass in beautiful gorge country. Kayak the pools and cast surface lures tight to the banks.

What is the best bait at Clarence River?

Good baits here are Live shrimp, Crickets / cicadas, Sweet corn, Bread / dough balls. For Australian Bass, try Live shrimp, Crickets / cicadas, Scrub worms.

When is the best time to fish Clarence River?

The best months are usually Oct–Mar. On the day, dawn and dusk and the hours around the tide change are typically best. CastCall scores Clarence River every hour for the next two weeks using tides, wind, swell, water temperature and the moon.
