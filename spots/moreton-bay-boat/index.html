Moreton Bay (boat) fishing

A harbour and bay in Brisbane & Moreton Bay, Queensland – boat / kayak. Huge sheltered bay. Snapper on the inshore reefs and wrecks, whiting on the sand banks, school mackerel in summer.

Common questions

What fish can you catch at Moreton Bay?

Moreton Bay holds Snapper, Yellowfin Bream, Dusky Flathead, Sand Whiting, Squid, Spotted & Spanish Mackerel and Tailor. Huge sheltered bay. Snapper on the inshore reefs and wrecks, whiting on the sand banks, school mackerel in summer.

What is the best bait at Moreton Bay?

Good baits here are Pilchard (unweighted floater), Squid or cuttlefish strips, Peeled prawn, Live nippers (yabbies), Live poddy mullet, Whole prawn. For Snapper, try Pilchard (unweighted floater), Squid or cuttlefish strips, Fresh yakka / slimy mackerel fillet.

When is the best time to fish Moreton Bay?

The best months are usually Feb–May. On the day, dawn and dusk and the hours around the tide change are typically best. CastCall scores Moreton Bay every hour for the next two weeks using tides, wind, swell, water temperature and the moon.
