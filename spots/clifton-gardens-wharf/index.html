Clifton Gardens Wharf fishing

A harbour and bay in Sydney, New South Wales – land-based. One of the best land-based squid spots in the harbour – weed beds either side of the wharf. Kings cruise past at dawn in summer.

Depth & structure

Within about 450 m of Clifton Gardens Wharf the seabed reaches about 24 m deep, with a drop-off from about 2 m to 21 m – drop-offs and holes like this are where fish hold, so fish the edges. Open the live forecast for the full depth map, deep holes and reefs (NSW Government 2018 survey).

Getting there

Paid parking at Clifton Gardens Reserve. Busy on summer weekends – go early.

Common questions

What fish can you catch at Clifton Gardens Wharf?

Clifton Gardens Wharf holds Squid, Yellowfin Bream, Silver Trevally, Tailor and Yellowtail Kingfish. One of the best land-based squid spots in the harbour – weed beds either side of the wharf. Kings cruise past at dawn in summer.

What is the best bait at Clifton Gardens Wharf?

Good baits here are Pilchard / mullet on a squid spike, Live yellowtail on a squid jag, Peeled prawn, Live nippers (yabbies), Pilchard pieces, Pilchard on gang hooks. For Squid, try Pilchard / mullet on a squid spike, Live yellowtail on a squid jag.

When is the best time to fish Clifton Gardens Wharf?

The best months are usually Apr–Aug. On the day, dawn and dusk and the hours around the tide change are typically best. CastCall scores Clifton Gardens Wharf every hour for the next two weeks using tides, wind, swell, water temperature and the moon.
