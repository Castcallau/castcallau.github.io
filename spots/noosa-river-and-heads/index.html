Noosa River & Heads fishing

An estuary in Sunshine Coast, Queensland – land-based and boat / kayak. Clear sand-bottomed river with big flathead. Trevally and jacks around the rock walls and snags.

Common questions

What fish can you catch at Noosa River & Heads?

Noosa River & Heads holds Dusky Flathead, Yellowfin Bream, Sand Whiting, Silver Trevally, Mangrove Jack and Tailor. Clear sand-bottomed river with big flathead. Trevally and jacks around the rock walls and snags.

What is the best bait at Noosa River & Heads?

Good baits here are Live poddy mullet, Whole prawn, Peeled prawn, Live nippers (yabbies), Beach worms, Bloodworms. For Dusky Flathead, try Live poddy mullet, Whole prawn, Pilchard / whitebait.

When is the best time to fish Noosa River & Heads?

The best months are usually Nov–Apr. On the day, dawn and dusk and the hours around the tide change are typically best. CastCall scores Noosa River & Heads every hour for the next two weeks using tides, wind, swell, water temperature and the moon.
