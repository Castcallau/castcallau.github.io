The Entrance – Tuggerah Channel fishing

An estuary in Central Coast, New South Wales – land-based. Fast tidal channel. Flathead and whiting on the sand, luderick and bream around the rock walls, tailor at the mouth.

Depth & structure

Within about 450 m of The Entrance the seabed reaches about 5 m deep, with a drop-off from about 0 m to 4 m – drop-offs and holes like this are where fish hold, so fish the edges. Open the live forecast for the full depth map, deep holes and reefs (NSW Government 2018 survey).

Getting there

Parking near the Memorial Park. Flows hard – use enough weight.

Common questions

What fish can you catch at The Entrance?

The Entrance holds Dusky Flathead, Sand Whiting, Yellowfin Bream, Luderick, Tailor and Mulloway. Fast tidal channel. Flathead and whiting on the sand, luderick and bream around the rock walls, tailor at the mouth.

What is the best bait at The Entrance?

Good baits here are Live poddy mullet, Whole prawn, Beach worms, Bloodworms, Peeled prawn, Live nippers (yabbies). For Dusky Flathead, try Live poddy mullet, Whole prawn, Pilchard / whitebait.

When is the best time to fish The Entrance?

The best months are usually Nov–May. On the day, dawn and dusk and the hours around the tide change are typically best. CastCall scores The Entrance every hour for the next two weeks using tides, wind, swell, water temperature and the moon.
