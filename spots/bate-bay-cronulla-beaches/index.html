Bate Bay – Cronulla Beaches fishing

A surf beach in Sydney, New South Wales – land-based. Long surf beach from Cronulla to Wanda. Salmon and tailor in the gutters in winter, whiting in summer, mulloway at night.

Depth & structure

Within about 450 m of Bate Bay the seabed reaches about 4 m deep, with a drop-off from about 1 m to 3 m – drop-offs and holes like this are where fish hold, so fish the edges. Open the live forecast for the full depth map, deep holes and reefs (NSW Government 2018 survey).

Getting there

Parking at Wanda and Elouera. Stay away from the patrolled swim areas.

Common questions

What fish can you catch at Bate Bay?

Bate Bay holds Australian Salmon, Tailor, Sand Whiting, Yellowfin Bream and Mulloway. Long surf beach from Cronulla to Wanda. Salmon and tailor in the gutters in winter, whiting in summer, mulloway at night.

What is the best bait at Bate Bay?

Good baits here are Pilchard on gang hooks, Beach worms, Garfish, Bloodworms, Peeled prawn, Live nippers (yabbies). For Australian Salmon, try Pilchard on gang hooks, Beach worms, Peeled prawn.

When is the best time to fish Bate Bay?

The best months are usually Mar–Jun. On the day, dawn and dusk and the hours around the tide change are typically best. CastCall scores Bate Bay every hour for the next two weeks using tides, wind, swell, water temperature and the moon.
