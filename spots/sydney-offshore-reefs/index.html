Common questions

What fish can you catch at Sydney Offshore Reefs?

Sydney Offshore Reefs holds Snapper, Yellowtail Kingfish, Tiger Flathead, Mahi Mahi and Australian Bonito. Reefs off North and South Head and Long Reef. The Sydney FADs hold mahi mahi in late summer when warm water pushes in.

What is the best bait at Sydney Offshore Reefs?

Good baits here are Pilchard (unweighted floater), Squid or cuttlefish strips, Live squid, Live yellowtail (yakkas) or slimy mackerel, Squid strips, Pilchard / mullet strips. For Snapper, try Pilchard (unweighted floater), Squid or cuttlefish strips, Fresh yakka / slimy mackerel fillet.

When is the best time to fish Sydney Offshore Reefs?

The best months are usually Jan–May. On the day, dawn and dusk and the hours around the tide change are typically best. CastCall scores Sydney Offshore Reefs every hour for the next two weeks using tides, wind, swell, water temperature and the moon.
