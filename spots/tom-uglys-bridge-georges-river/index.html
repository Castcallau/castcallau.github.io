Common questions

What fish can you catch at Tom Ugly's Bridge?

Tom Ugly's Bridge holds Yellowfin Bream, Dusky Flathead, Mulloway and Sand Whiting. Another classic Georges River bridge. Bream around the pylons, flathead along the drop-offs on the run-out.

What is the best bait at Tom Ugly's Bridge?

Good baits here are Peeled prawn, Live nippers (yabbies), Live poddy mullet, Whole prawn, Live mullet / yellowtail / squid, Fresh squid strips or heads. For Yellowfin Bream, try Peeled prawn, Live nippers (yabbies), Mullet / tuna strip.

When is the best time to fish Tom Ugly's Bridge?

The best months are usually Nov–Mar. On the day, dawn and dusk and the hours around the tide change are typically best. CastCall scores Tom Ugly's Bridge every hour for the next two weeks using tides, wind, swell, water temperature and the moon.
