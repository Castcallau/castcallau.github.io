Western Port – Flinders & San Remo fishing

A harbour and bay in Western Port & Phillip Island, Victoria – land-based and boat / kayak. Big tides and deep channels. Snapper and gummy shark in the channels, whiting and squid on the banks.

Common questions

What fish can you catch at Western Port?

Western Port holds Snapper, King George Whiting, Squid, Australian Salmon and Sand Flathead. Big tides and deep channels. Snapper and gummy shark in the channels, whiting and squid on the banks.

What is the best bait at Western Port?

Good baits here are Pilchard (unweighted floater), Squid or cuttlefish strips, Pipis, Squid strips, Pilchard / mullet on a squid spike, Live yellowtail on a squid jag. For Snapper, try Pilchard (unweighted floater), Squid or cuttlefish strips, Fresh yakka / slimy mackerel fillet.

When is the best time to fish Western Port?

The best months are usually Mar–Jun, Sep. On the day, dawn and dusk and the hours around the tide change are typically best. CastCall scores Western Port every hour for the next two weeks using tides, wind, swell, water temperature and the moon.
