Great Barrier Reef – off Cairns (boat) fishing

An offshore reef in Far North Queensland, Queensland – boat / kayak. World-famous reef fishing. Coral trout on the bommies, Spanish mackerel on the reef edges in winter–spring.

Common questions

What fish can you catch at Great Barrier Reef?

Great Barrier Reef holds Coral Trout, Spotted & Spanish Mackerel, Silver Trevally and Mahi Mahi. World-famous reef fishing. Coral trout on the bommies, Spanish mackerel on the reef edges in winter–spring.

What is the best bait at Great Barrier Reef?

Good baits here are Whole pilchard, Live fusilier / hardiheads, Live slimy mackerel / yakka, Whole garfish or pilchard trolled slowly, Peeled prawn, Pilchard pieces. For Coral Trout, try Whole pilchard, Live fusilier / hardiheads, Squid.

When is the best time to fish Great Barrier Reef?

The best months are usually Feb–May. On the day, dawn and dusk and the hours around the tide change are typically best. CastCall scores Great Barrier Reef every hour for the next two weeks using tides, wind, swell, water temperature and the moon.
