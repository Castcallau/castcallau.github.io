Byron Bay – The Pass & Cape Byron fishing

A rock platform in North Coast, New South Wales – land-based and boat / kayak. North-facing bay sheltered from southerlies. Mackerel and trevally in summer, tailor in winter off the rocks at The Pass.

Depth & structure

Within about 450 m of Byron Bay the seabed reaches about 8 m deep, with a drop-off from about 1 m to 5 m – drop-offs and holes like this are where fish hold, so fish the edges. Open the live forecast for the full depth map, deep holes and reefs (NSW Government 2018 survey).

Getting there

Cape Byron Marine Park – check sanctuary zones before you fish. Parking is busy.

Common questions

What fish can you catch at Byron Bay?

Byron Bay holds Tailor, Spotted & Spanish Mackerel, Silver Trevally, Yellowfin Bream and Yellowtail Kingfish. North-facing bay sheltered from southerlies. Mackerel and trevally in summer, tailor in winter off the rocks at The Pass.

What is the best bait at Byron Bay?

Good baits here are Pilchard on gang hooks, Garfish, Live slimy mackerel / yakka, Whole garfish or pilchard trolled slowly, Peeled prawn, Pilchard pieces. For Tailor, try Pilchard on gang hooks, Garfish, Fresh bonito / mullet strip.

When is the best time to fish Byron Bay?

The best months are usually Mar–Jun. On the day, dawn and dusk and the hours around the tide change are typically best. CastCall scores Byron Bay every hour for the next two weeks using tides, wind, swell, water temperature and the moon.
