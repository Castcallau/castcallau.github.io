South West Rocks – Trial Bay fishing

A rock platform in Mid North Coast, New South Wales – land-based and boat / kayak. North-facing bay, sheltered from southerlies. Fish Rock and Green Island offshore for mackerel and kings.

Depth & structure

Within about 450 m of South West Rocks the seabed reaches about 23 m deep, with a drop-off from about 8 m to 11 m – drop-offs and holes like this are where fish hold, so fish the edges. Open the live forecast for the full depth map, deep holes and reefs (NSW Government 2018 survey).

Getting there

Arakoon / Trial Bay Gaol area – national park fees apply.

Common questions

What fish can you catch at South West Rocks?

South West Rocks holds Snapper, Tailor, Mulloway, Yellowfin Bream, Yellowtail Kingfish and Spotted & Spanish Mackerel. North-facing bay, sheltered from southerlies. Fish Rock and Green Island offshore for mackerel and kings.

What is the best bait at South West Rocks?

Good baits here are Pilchard (unweighted floater), Squid or cuttlefish strips, Pilchard on gang hooks, Garfish, Live mullet / yellowtail / squid, Fresh squid strips or heads. For Snapper, try Pilchard (unweighted floater), Squid or cuttlefish strips, Fresh yakka / slimy mackerel fillet.

When is the best time to fish South West Rocks?

The best months are usually Feb–May. On the day, dawn and dusk and the hours around the tide change are typically best. CastCall scores South West Rocks every hour for the next two weeks using tides, wind, swell, water temperature and the moon.
