Lake Tinaroo – Atherton Tablelands fishing

A dam / lake in Far North Queensland, Queensland – boat / kayak and land-based. Stocked impoundment famous for metre-plus barra. Lures around the timber and weed edges on warm nights.

Common questions

What fish can you catch at Lake Tinaroo?

Lake Tinaroo holds Barramundi. Stocked impoundment famous for metre-plus barra. Lures around the timber and weed edges on warm nights.

What is the best bait at Lake Tinaroo?

Good baits here are Live mullet, Live prawns. For Barramundi, try Live mullet, Live prawns, Live cherabin (freshwater prawns).

When is the best time to fish Lake Tinaroo?

The best months are usually Oct–Mar. On the day, dawn and dusk and the hours around the tide change are typically best. CastCall scores Lake Tinaroo every hour for the next two weeks using tides, wind, swell, water temperature and the moon.
