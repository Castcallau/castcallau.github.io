Common questions

What fish can you catch at Mandurah Estuary?

Mandurah Estuary holds Black Bream, Tailor, Australian Herring, Sand Whiting, Mulloway and Garfish. Estuary channels and bridges hold bream and tailor. Herring and whiting at the mouth.

What is the best bait at Mandurah Estuary?

Good baits here are Sandworm / bloodworm, Spider crab, Pilchard on gang hooks, Garfish, Maggots / wogs under a float, Prawn pieces. For Black Bream, try Sandworm / bloodworm, Spider crab, Peeled prawn.

When is the best time to fish Mandurah Estuary?

The best months are usually Mar–May, Nov. On the day, dawn and dusk and the hours around the tide change are typically best. CastCall scores Mandurah Estuary every hour for the next two weeks using tides, wind, swell, water temperature and the moon.
