Norah Head Rocks fishing

A rock platform in Central Coast, New South Wales – land-based. Productive washes either side of the lighthouse. Snapper and kings on floating baits when there is a bit of swell.

Depth & structure

Within about 450 m of Norah Head Rocks the seabed reaches about 18 m deep, with a drop-off from about 0 m to 10 m – drop-offs and holes like this are where fish hold, so fish the edges. Open the live forecast for the full depth map, deep holes and reefs (NSW Government 2018 survey).

Getting there

Rock fishing – lifejacket strongly advised. Never fish here in big swell.

Common questions

What fish can you catch at Norah Head Rocks?

Norah Head Rocks holds Black Drummer, Luderick, Australian Salmon, Tailor, Snapper and Yellowtail Kingfish. Productive washes either side of the lighthouse. Snapper and kings on floating baits when there is a bit of swell.

What is the best bait at Norah Head Rocks?

Good baits here are Cabbage weed, Bread, Green weed (tidal ribbon weed), Cabbage weed (on the rocks), Pilchard on gang hooks, Beach worms. For Black Drummer, try Cabbage weed, Bread, Peeled prawn.

When is the best time to fish Norah Head Rocks?

The best months are usually Apr–Jul. On the day, dawn and dusk and the hours around the tide change are typically best. CastCall scores Norah Head Rocks every hour for the next two weeks using tides, wind, swell, water temperature and the moon.
