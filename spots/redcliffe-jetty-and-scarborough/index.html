Common questions

What fish can you catch at Redcliffe Jetty & Scarborough?

Redcliffe Jetty & Scarborough holds Yellowfin Bream, Sand Whiting, Dusky Flathead, Squid, Snapper and Mangrove Jack. Popular land-based spot. Bream and whiting around the jetty, squid at dusk, the odd snapper after dark.

What is the best bait at Redcliffe Jetty & Scarborough?

Good baits here are Peeled prawn, Live nippers (yabbies), Beach worms, Bloodworms, Live poddy mullet, Whole prawn. For Yellowfin Bream, try Peeled prawn, Live nippers (yabbies), Mullet / tuna strip.

When is the best time to fish Redcliffe Jetty & Scarborough?

The best months are usually Nov–Mar. On the day, dawn and dusk and the hours around the tide change are typically best. CastCall scores Redcliffe Jetty & Scarborough every hour for the next two weeks using tides, wind, swell, water temperature and the moon.
