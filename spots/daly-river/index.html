Common questions

What fish can you catch at Daly River?

Daly River holds Barramundi. Big, snag-filled river with a famous barra run in the early dry season.

What is the best bait at Daly River?

Good baits here are Live mullet, Live prawns. For Barramundi, try Live mullet, Live prawns, Live cherabin (freshwater prawns).

When is the best time to fish Daly River?

The best months are usually Oct–Mar. On the day, dawn and dusk and the hours around the tide change are typically best. CastCall scores Daly River every hour for the next two weeks using tides, wind, swell, water temperature and the moon.
