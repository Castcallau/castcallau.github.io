Common questions

What fish can you catch at Perth Beaches?

Perth Beaches holds Australian Herring, Sand Whiting, Tailor, Australian Salmon and Mulloway. Herring and whiting in the shallows, tailor at dusk, and the big autumn salmon run.

What is the best bait at Perth Beaches?

Good baits here are Maggots / wogs under a float, Prawn pieces, Beach worms, Bloodworms, Pilchard on gang hooks, Garfish. For Australian Herring, try Maggots / wogs under a float, Prawn pieces, Squid strips.

When is the best time to fish Perth Beaches?

The best months are usually Mar–Jun. On the day, dawn and dusk and the hours around the tide change are typically best. CastCall scores Perth Beaches every hour for the next two weeks using tides, wind, swell, water temperature and the moon.
