Ballina – Richmond River Breakwall fishing

A breakwall in North Coast, New South Wales – land-based and boat / kayak. Mulloway, tailor and bream off the walls. Summer brings mackerel and trevally in close.

Depth & structure

Within about 450 m of Ballina the seabed reaches about 9 m deep, with a drop-off from about 3 m to 8 m – drop-offs and holes like this are where fish hold, so fish the edges. Open the live forecast for the full depth map, deep holes and reefs (NSW Government 2018 survey).

Getting there

Lighthouse Beach / South Wall access. The Ballina bar is dangerous – boats check conditions.

Common questions

What fish can you catch at Ballina?

Ballina holds Mulloway, Tailor, Yellowfin Bream, Dusky Flathead, Silver Trevally and Spotted & Spanish Mackerel. Mulloway, tailor and bream off the walls. Summer brings mackerel and trevally in close.

What is the best bait at Ballina?

Good baits here are Live mullet / yellowtail / squid, Fresh squid strips or heads, Pilchard on gang hooks, Garfish, Peeled prawn, Live nippers (yabbies). For Mulloway, try Live mullet / yellowtail / squid, Fresh squid strips or heads, Fresh tailor / bonito fillet.

When is the best time to fish Ballina?

The best months are usually Feb–Jun. On the day, dawn and dusk and the hours around the tide change are typically best. CastCall scores Ballina every hour for the next two weeks using tides, wind, swell, water temperature and the moon.
