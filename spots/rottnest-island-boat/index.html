Common questions

What fish can you catch at Rottnest Island?

Rottnest Island holds WA Dhufish, Snapper, Yellowtail Kingfish, Australian Herring, Squid and King George Whiting. Squid and whiting in the bays, kingfish and snapper on the reefs, dhufish on the deeper lumps.

What is the best bait at Rottnest Island?

Good baits here are Octopus, Squid, Pilchard (unweighted floater), Squid or cuttlefish strips, Live squid, Live yellowtail (yakkas) or slimy mackerel. For WA Dhufish, try Octopus, Squid, Fresh mulie (pilchard) or fish fillet.

When is the best time to fish Rottnest Island?

The best months are usually Mar–Jun. On the day, dawn and dusk and the hours around the tide change are typically best. CastCall scores Rottnest Island every hour for the next two weeks using tides, wind, swell, water temperature and the moon.
