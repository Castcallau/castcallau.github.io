Cronulla – Oak Park & Shelly Park Rocks fishing

A rock platform in Sydney, New South Wales – land-based. Low rock ledges on the Cronulla peninsula. Luderick and drummer in the washes, salmon and tailor at first light.

Depth & structure

Within about 450 m of Cronulla the seabed reaches about 12 m deep, with a drop-off from about 2 m to 9 m – drop-offs and holes like this are where fish hold, so fish the edges. Open the live forecast for the full depth map, deep holes and reefs (NSW Government 2018 survey).

Getting there

Parking at Oak Park. Low platforms – check the swell and wear a lifejacket.

Common questions

What fish can you catch at Cronulla?

Cronulla holds Black Drummer, Luderick, Yellowfin Bream, Australian Salmon, Tailor and Squid. Low rock ledges on the Cronulla peninsula. Luderick and drummer in the washes, salmon and tailor at first light.

What is the best bait at Cronulla?

Good baits here are Cabbage weed, Bread, Green weed (tidal ribbon weed), Cabbage weed (on the rocks), Peeled prawn, Live nippers (yabbies). For Black Drummer, try Cabbage weed, Bread, Peeled prawn.

When is the best time to fish Cronulla?

The best months are usually May–Aug. On the day, dawn and dusk and the hours around the tide change are typically best. CastCall scores Cronulla every hour for the next two weeks using tides, wind, swell, water temperature and the moon.
