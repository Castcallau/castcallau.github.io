Common questions

What fish can you catch at Port Lincoln?

Port Lincoln holds King George Whiting, Snapper, Squid, Australian Salmon, Yellowtail Kingfish and Australian Herring. Boston Bay has whiting and squid, kingfish around the islands. Salmon off the surf beaches.

What is the best bait at Port Lincoln?

Good baits here are Pipis, Squid strips, Pilchard (unweighted floater), Squid or cuttlefish strips, Pilchard / mullet on a squid spike, Live yellowtail on a squid jag. For King George Whiting, try Pipis, Squid strips, Mussel.

When is the best time to fish Port Lincoln?

The best months are usually Mar–Jun. On the day, dawn and dusk and the hours around the tide change are typically best. CastCall scores Port Lincoln every hour for the next two weeks using tides, wind, swell, water temperature and the moon.
