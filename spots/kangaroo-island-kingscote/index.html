Common questions

What fish can you catch at Kangaroo Island?

Kangaroo Island holds King George Whiting, Squid, Australian Salmon, Snapper, Australian Herring and Garfish. Bays full of whiting and squid, salmon on the beaches. Very little fishing pressure.

What is the best bait at Kangaroo Island?

Good baits here are Pipis, Squid strips, Pilchard / mullet on a squid spike, Live yellowtail on a squid jag, Pilchard on gang hooks, Beach worms. For King George Whiting, try Pipis, Squid strips, Mussel.

When is the best time to fish Kangaroo Island?

The best months are usually Mar–Aug. On the day, dawn and dusk and the hours around the tide change are typically best. CastCall scores Kangaroo Island every hour for the next two weeks using tides, wind, swell, water temperature and the moon.
