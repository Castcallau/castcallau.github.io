Terrigal Haven fishing

A rock platform in Central Coast, New South Wales – land-based and boat / kayak. Sheltered haven below the Skillion. Bream and snapper off the rocks at dusk, squid around the boat ramp.

Depth & structure

Within about 450 m of Terrigal Haven the seabed reaches about 18 m deep, with a drop-off from about 8 m to 18 m – drop-offs and holes like this are where fish hold, so fish the edges. Open the live forecast for the full depth map, deep holes and reefs (NSW Government 2018 survey).

Getting there

Parking at The Haven. Busy boat ramp – fish away from it.

Common questions

What fish can you catch at Terrigal Haven?

Terrigal Haven holds Yellowfin Bream, Black Drummer, Luderick, Snapper, Squid and Australian Salmon. Sheltered haven below the Skillion. Bream and snapper off the rocks at dusk, squid around the boat ramp.

What is the best bait at Terrigal Haven?

Good baits here are Peeled prawn, Live nippers (yabbies), Cabbage weed, Bread, Green weed (tidal ribbon weed), Cabbage weed (on the rocks). For Yellowfin Bream, try Peeled prawn, Live nippers (yabbies), Mullet / tuna strip.

When is the best time to fish Terrigal Haven?

The best months are usually May–Aug. On the day, dawn and dusk and the hours around the tide change are typically best. CastCall scores Terrigal Haven every hour for the next two weeks using tides, wind, swell, water temperature and the moon.
