Common questions

What fish can you catch at Swan River?

Swan River holds Black Bream, Mulloway, Dusky Flathead, Australian Herring and Tailor. Black bream along the jetties and snags, tailor and herring near Fremantle, mulloway in the deep holes at night.

What is the best bait at Swan River?

Good baits here are Sandworm / bloodworm, Spider crab, Live mullet / yellowtail / squid, Fresh squid strips or heads, Live poddy mullet, Whole prawn. For Black Bream, try Sandworm / bloodworm, Spider crab, Peeled prawn.

When is the best time to fish Swan River?

The best months are usually Mar–Jun, Nov. On the day, dawn and dusk and the hours around the tide change are typically best. CastCall scores Swan River every hour for the next two weeks using tides, wind, swell, water temperature and the moon.
