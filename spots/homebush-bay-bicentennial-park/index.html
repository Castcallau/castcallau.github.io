Homebush Bay – Bicentennial Park fishing

An estuary in Sydney, New South Wales – land-based. Close to Strathfield. Bream and flathead along the seawalls and mangroves on the tide. Strictly catch and release (see health warning).

Common questions

What fish can you catch at Homebush Bay?

Homebush Bay holds Yellowfin Bream, Dusky Flathead, Mulloway and European Carp. Close to Strathfield. Bream and flathead along the seawalls and mangroves on the tide. Strictly catch and release (see health warning).

What is the best bait at Homebush Bay?

Good baits here are Peeled prawn, Live nippers (yabbies), Live poddy mullet, Whole prawn, Live mullet / yellowtail / squid, Fresh squid strips or heads. For Yellowfin Bream, try Peeled prawn, Live nippers (yabbies), Mullet / tuna strip.

When is the best time to fish Homebush Bay?

The best months are usually Nov–Mar. On the day, dawn and dusk and the hours around the tide change are typically best. CastCall scores Homebush Bay every hour for the next two weeks using tides, wind, swell, water temperature and the moon.
