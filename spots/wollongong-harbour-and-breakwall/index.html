Wollongong Harbour & Breakwall fishing

A harbour and bay in Illawarra & South Coast, New South Wales – land-based and boat / kayak. The breakwall and Belmore Basin hold trevally, bream and squid. Salmon and tailor off the lighthouse end.

Depth & structure

Within about 450 m of Wollongong Harbour & Breakwall the seabed reaches about 11 m deep, with a drop-off from about 3 m to 8 m – drop-offs and holes like this are where fish hold, so fish the edges. Open the live forecast for the full depth map, deep holes and reefs (NSW Government 2018 survey).

Common questions

What fish can you catch at Wollongong Harbour & Breakwall?

Wollongong Harbour & Breakwall holds Yellowfin Bream, Silver Trevally, Squid, Australian Salmon, Tailor and Yellowtail Kingfish. The breakwall and Belmore Basin hold trevally, bream and squid. Salmon and tailor off the lighthouse end.

What is the best bait at Wollongong Harbour & Breakwall?

Good baits here are Peeled prawn, Live nippers (yabbies), Pilchard pieces, Pilchard / mullet on a squid spike, Live yellowtail on a squid jag, Pilchard on gang hooks. For Yellowfin Bream, try Peeled prawn, Live nippers (yabbies), Mullet / tuna strip.

When is the best time to fish Wollongong Harbour & Breakwall?

The best months are usually Apr–Aug. On the day, dawn and dusk and the hours around the tide change are typically best. CastCall scores Wollongong Harbour & Breakwall every hour for the next two weeks using tides, wind, swell, water temperature and the moon.
