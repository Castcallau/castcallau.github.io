Common questions

What fish can you catch at Copeton Dam?

Copeton Dam holds Murray Cod, Golden Perch and European Carp. Famous for trophy Murray cod. Big spinnerbaits and swimbaits around the granite boulders and timber.

What is the best bait at Copeton Dam?

Good baits here are Live yabbies, Bardi grubs, Live shrimp, Sweet corn, Bread / dough balls. For Murray Cod, try Live yabbies, Bardi grubs, Big scrub worm bunches.

When is the best time to fish Copeton Dam?

The best months are usually Dec–Mar. On the day, dawn and dusk and the hours around the tide change are typically best. CastCall scores Copeton Dam every hour for the next two weeks using tides, wind, swell, water temperature and the moon.
