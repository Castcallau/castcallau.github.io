Jervis Bay – Huskisson & Currarong fishing

A harbour and bay in Illawarra & South Coast, New South Wales – boat / kayak and land-based. Crystal-clear water – squid over the weed beds, snapper and kings around the bay. Currarong rocks for salmon.

Common questions

What fish can you catch at Jervis Bay?

Jervis Bay holds Snapper, Squid, Yellowtail Kingfish, Dusky Flathead, Sand Whiting and Australian Salmon. Crystal-clear water – squid over the weed beds, snapper and kings around the bay. Currarong rocks for salmon.

What is the best bait at Jervis Bay?

Good baits here are Pilchard (unweighted floater), Squid or cuttlefish strips, Pilchard / mullet on a squid spike, Live yellowtail on a squid jag, Live squid, Live yellowtail (yakkas) or slimy mackerel. For Snapper, try Pilchard (unweighted floater), Squid or cuttlefish strips, Fresh yakka / slimy mackerel fillet.

When is the best time to fish Jervis Bay?

The best months are usually Nov–Apr. On the day, dawn and dusk and the hours around the tide change are typically best. CastCall scores Jervis Bay every hour for the next two weeks using tides, wind, swell, water temperature and the moon.
