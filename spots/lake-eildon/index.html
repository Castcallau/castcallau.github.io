Common questions

What fish can you catch at Lake Eildon?

Lake Eildon holds Murray Cod, Golden Perch, Trout, Redfin and European Carp. Victoria's big inland lake with cod, yellowbelly, trout and redfin.

What is the best bait at Lake Eildon?

Good baits here are Live yabbies, Bardi grubs, Live shrimp, Scrub worms, Mudeyes under a float, Small yabbies. For Murray Cod, try Live yabbies, Bardi grubs, Big scrub worm bunches.

When is the best time to fish Lake Eildon?

The best months are usually Jan–Apr. On the day, dawn and dusk and the hours around the tide change are typically best. CastCall scores Lake Eildon every hour for the next two weeks using tides, wind, swell, water temperature and the moon.
