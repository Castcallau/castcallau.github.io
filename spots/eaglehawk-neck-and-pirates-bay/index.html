Common questions

What fish can you catch at Eaglehawk Neck & Pirates Bay?

Eaglehawk Neck & Pirates Bay holds Australian Salmon, Squid and Sand Flathead. Rugged coast with salmon off the rocks and beach, squid in the bay.

What is the best bait at Eaglehawk Neck & Pirates Bay?

Good baits here are Pilchard on gang hooks, Beach worms, Pilchard / mullet on a squid spike, Live yellowtail on a squid jag, Squid strips, Pilchard pieces. For Australian Salmon, try Pilchard on gang hooks, Beach worms, Peeled prawn.

When is the best time to fish Eaglehawk Neck & Pirates Bay?
