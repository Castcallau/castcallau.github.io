Common questions

What fish can you catch at Glenbawn Dam?

Glenbawn Dam holds Australian Bass, Golden Perch, Murray Cod and European Carp. Big Hunter Valley dam with bass, yellowbelly and cod. Lipless crankbaits and blades on the schooling bass.

What is the best bait at Glenbawn Dam?

Good baits here are Live shrimp, Crickets / cicadas, Live yabbies, Bardi grubs, Sweet corn, Bread / dough balls. For Australian Bass, try Live shrimp, Crickets / cicadas, Scrub worms.

When is the best time to fish Glenbawn Dam?

The best months are usually Dec–Mar. On the day, dawn and dusk and the hours around the tide change are typically best. CastCall scores Glenbawn Dam every hour for the next two weeks using tides, wind, swell, water temperature and the moon.
