Depth & structure

Within about 450 m of Narrabeen Lake Entrance the seabed reaches about 21 m deep, with a drop-off from about 11 m to 17 m – drop-offs and holes like this are where fish hold, so fish the edges. Open the live forecast for the full depth map, deep holes and reefs (NSW Government 2018 survey).

Common questions

What fish can you catch at Narrabeen Lake Entrance?

Narrabeen Lake Entrance holds Dusky Flathead, Sand Whiting, Yellowfin Bream and Luderick. Wade the sand flats inside the entrance. Flathead lie along the channel edge on the run-out.

What is the best bait at Narrabeen Lake Entrance?

Good baits here are Live poddy mullet, Whole prawn, Beach worms, Bloodworms, Peeled prawn, Live nippers (yabbies). For Dusky Flathead, try Live poddy mullet, Whole prawn, Pilchard / whitebait.

When is the best time to fish Narrabeen Lake Entrance?
