Hawkesbury River – Brooklyn fishing

An estuary in Sydney, New South Wales – boat / kayak and land-based. Famous mulloway water – the railway bridge pylons and deep holes around Juno Point. Flathead on the sand edges.

Common questions

What fish can you catch at Hawkesbury River?

Hawkesbury River holds Mulloway, Dusky Flathead, Yellowfin Bream, Yellowtail Kingfish and Squid. Famous mulloway water – the railway bridge pylons and deep holes around Juno Point. Flathead on the sand edges.

What is the best bait at Hawkesbury River?

Good baits here are Live mullet / yellowtail / squid, Fresh squid strips or heads, Live poddy mullet, Whole prawn, Peeled prawn, Live nippers (yabbies). For Mulloway, try Live mullet / yellowtail / squid, Fresh squid strips or heads, Fresh tailor / bonito fillet.

When is the best time to fish Hawkesbury River?

The best months are usually Nov–Mar. On the day, dawn and dusk and the hours around the tide change are typically best. CastCall scores Hawkesbury River every hour for the next two weeks using tides, wind, swell, water temperature and the moon.
