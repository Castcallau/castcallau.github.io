Common questions

What fish can you catch at Esperance?

Esperance holds Australian Salmon, Australian Herring, King George Whiting, Squid and Garfish. White sand beaches and clear water. Salmon in the surf, herring and squid around town.

What is the best bait at Esperance?

Good baits here are Pilchard on gang hooks, Beach worms, Maggots / wogs under a float, Prawn pieces, Pipis, Squid strips. For Australian Salmon, try Pilchard on gang hooks, Beach worms, Peeled prawn.

When is the best time to fish Esperance?

The best months are usually Mar–Jun. On the day, dawn and dusk and the hours around the tide change are typically best. CastCall scores Esperance every hour for the next two weeks using tides, wind, swell, water temperature and the moon.
