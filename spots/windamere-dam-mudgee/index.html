Windamere Dam – Mudgee fishing

A dam / lake in Blue Mountains & Central West, New South Wales – land-based and boat / kayak. Clear, timbered dam with good yellowbelly and cod numbers. Spinnerbaits along the timber lines at dawn.

Common questions

What fish can you catch at Windamere Dam?

Windamere Dam holds Golden Perch, Murray Cod and Redfin. Clear, timbered dam with good yellowbelly and cod numbers. Spinnerbaits along the timber lines at dawn.

What is the best bait at Windamere Dam?

Good baits here are Live yabbies, Live shrimp, Bardi grubs, Scrub worms, Small yabbies. For Golden Perch, try Live yabbies, Live shrimp, Scrub worms.

When is the best time to fish Windamere Dam?

The best months are usually Jan–Apr. On the day, dawn and dusk and the hours around the tide change are typically best. CastCall scores Windamere Dam every hour for the next two weeks using tides, wind, swell, water temperature and the moon.
