Lake Mulwala – Murray River fishing

A dam / lake in Riverina & Murray, New South Wales – land-based and boat / kayak. Legendary Murray cod water full of drowned red gums. Spinnerbaits and big hardbodies cast at the trees.

Common questions

What fish can you catch at Lake Mulwala?

Lake Mulwala holds Murray Cod, Golden Perch, Redfin and European Carp. Legendary Murray cod water full of drowned red gums. Spinnerbaits and big hardbodies cast at the trees.

What is the best bait at Lake Mulwala?

Good baits here are Live yabbies, Bardi grubs, Live shrimp, Scrub worms, Small yabbies, Sweet corn. For Murray Cod, try Live yabbies, Bardi grubs, Big scrub worm bunches.

When is the best time to fish Lake Mulwala?

The best months are usually Dec–Apr. On the day, dawn and dusk and the hours around the tide change are typically best. CastCall scores Lake Mulwala every hour for the next two weeks using tides, wind, swell, water temperature and the moon.
