Common questions

What fish can you catch at Mallacoota Inlet?

Mallacoota Inlet holds Black Bream, Dusky Flathead, Tailor, Australian Salmon and Mulloway. Remote inlet with big flathead and bream. Salmon on the surf beaches.

What is the best bait at Mallacoota Inlet?

Good baits here are Sandworm / bloodworm, Spider crab, Live poddy mullet, Whole prawn, Pilchard on gang hooks, Garfish. For Black Bream, try Sandworm / bloodworm, Spider crab, Peeled prawn.

When is the best time to fish Mallacoota Inlet?

The best months are usually Mar–Jun, Sep, Nov. On the day, dawn and dusk and the hours around the tide change are typically best. CastCall scores Mallacoota Inlet every hour for the next two weeks using tides, wind, swell, water temperature and the moon.
