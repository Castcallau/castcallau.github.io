Exmouth – Ningaloo Reef fishing

An offshore reef in Gascoyne & Ningaloo, Western Australia – boat / kayak and land-based. World heritage reef. Coral trout and emperor inside, mackerel and trevally on the edges.

Common questions

What fish can you catch at Exmouth?

Exmouth holds Coral Trout, Spotted & Spanish Mackerel, Queenfish, Silver Trevally and Mahi Mahi. World heritage reef. Coral trout and emperor inside, mackerel and trevally on the edges.

What is the best bait at Exmouth?

Good baits here are Whole pilchard, Live fusilier / hardiheads, Live slimy mackerel / yakka, Whole garfish or pilchard trolled slowly, Live herring / mullet, Whole garfish. For Coral Trout, try Whole pilchard, Live fusilier / hardiheads, Squid.

When is the best time to fish Exmouth?

The best months are usually Feb–May. On the day, dawn and dusk and the hours around the tide change are typically best. CastCall scores Exmouth every hour for the next two weeks using tides, wind, swell, water temperature and the moon.
