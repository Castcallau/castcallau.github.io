Common questions

What fish can you catch at St Helens?

St Helens holds Black Bream, Sand Flathead, Australian Salmon, Squid and Garfish. Sheltered bay famous for black bream. Flathead and squid throughout.

What is the best bait at St Helens?

Good baits here are Sandworm / bloodworm, Spider crab, Squid strips, Pilchard pieces, Pilchard on gang hooks, Beach worms. For Black Bream, try Sandworm / bloodworm, Spider crab, Peeled prawn.

When is the best time to fish St Helens?

The best months are usually Mar–Jun, Sep–Oct. On the day, dawn and dusk and the hours around the tide change are typically best. CastCall scores St Helens every hour for the next two weeks using tides, wind, swell, water temperature and the moon.
