K'gari (Fraser Island) – Eastern Beach fishing

A surf beach in Fraser Coast, Queensland – land-based. Legendary surf beach. The winter tailor run along the gutters, whiting and dart in summer.

Common questions

What fish can you catch at K'gari?

K'gari holds Tailor, Sand Whiting, Yellowfin Bream, Mulloway and Silver Trevally. Legendary surf beach. The winter tailor run along the gutters, whiting and dart in summer.

What is the best bait at K'gari?

Good baits here are Pilchard on gang hooks, Garfish, Beach worms, Bloodworms, Peeled prawn, Live nippers (yabbies). For Tailor, try Pilchard on gang hooks, Garfish, Fresh bonito / mullet strip.

When is the best time to fish K'gari?

The best months are usually Mar–Jun. On the day, dawn and dusk and the hours around the tide change are typically best. CastCall scores K'gari every hour for the next two weeks using tides, wind, swell, water temperature and the moon.
