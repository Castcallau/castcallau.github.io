Brisbane Water – Woy Woy & Ettalong fishing

An estuary in Central Coast, New South Wales – land-based and boat / kayak. Sheltered bay with channels, weed beds and sand flats. Flathead along the drop-offs, bream around the wharves, squid on the weed.

JFMAMJJASOND

2–3" soft plastic grubs on 1/16–1/8oz jighead · Small deep-diving hardbody crankbaits · Surface poppers / walkers in summer

Running ball sinker (as light as possible) to a #1–1/0 hook on 6–8lb fluorocarbon. Unweighted baits drifted in a berley trail work best around structure.

Common questions

What fish can you catch at Brisbane Water?

Brisbane Water holds Dusky Flathead, Yellowfin Bream, Sand Whiting, Squid, Luderick and Mulloway. Sheltered bay with channels, weed beds and sand flats. Flathead along the drop-offs, bream around the wharves, squid on the weed.

What is the best bait at Brisbane Water?

Good baits here are Live poddy mullet, Whole prawn, Peeled prawn, Live nippers (yabbies), Beach worms, Bloodworms. For Dusky Flathead, try Live poddy mullet, Whole prawn, Pilchard / whitebait.

When is the best time to fish Brisbane Water?

The best months are usually Oct–Apr. On the day, dawn and dusk and the hours around the tide change are typically best. CastCall scores Brisbane Water every hour for the next two weeks using tides, wind, swell, water temperature and the moon.
