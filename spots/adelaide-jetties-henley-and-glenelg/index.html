Common questions

What fish can you catch at Adelaide Jetties?

Adelaide Jetties holds Squid, Australian Herring, Garfish, King George Whiting, Australian Salmon and Snapper. Metro jetties with squid, tommy ruff and garfish. Great for an evening session.

What is the best bait at Adelaide Jetties?

Good baits here are Pilchard / mullet on a squid spike, Live yellowtail on a squid jag, Maggots / wogs under a float, Prawn pieces, Bread, Maggots. For Squid, try Pilchard / mullet on a squid spike, Live yellowtail on a squid jag.

When is the best time to fish Adelaide Jetties?

The best months are usually Mar–Aug. On the day, dawn and dusk and the hours around the tide change are typically best. CastCall scores Adelaide Jetties every hour for the next two weeks using tides, wind, swell, water temperature and the moon.
