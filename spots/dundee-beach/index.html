Dundee Beach fishing

A rock platform in Top End, Northern Territory – land-based and boat / kayak. Rocky points and reefs west of Darwin. Queenies and trevally off the rocks, mackerel offshore.

⚠️ Crocodile country – stay well back from the water’s edge, never wade, and don’t clean fish at the water.

⚠️ Rock fishing is dangerous in swell – check conditions, watch the sets for 15 minutes first, and wear a lifejacket (compulsory at many NSW rock spots).

About 2.5 hours from Darwin. Crocodiles and box jellyfish – no swimming.

Common questions

What fish can you catch at Dundee Beach?

Dundee Beach holds Queenfish, Spotted & Spanish Mackerel, Silver Trevally and Barramundi. Rocky points and reefs west of Darwin. Queenies and trevally off the rocks, mackerel offshore.

What is the best bait at Dundee Beach?

Good baits here are Live herring / mullet, Whole garfish, Live slimy mackerel / yakka, Whole garfish or pilchard trolled slowly, Peeled prawn, Pilchard pieces. For Queenfish, try Live herring / mullet, Whole garfish.

When is the best time to fish Dundee Beach?

The best months are usually Feb–May. On the day, dawn and dusk and the hours around the tide change are typically best. CastCall scores Dundee Beach every hour for the next two weeks using tides, wind, swell, water temperature and the moon.
