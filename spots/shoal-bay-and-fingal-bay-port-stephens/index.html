Shoal Bay & Fingal Bay – Port Stephens fishing

A surf beach in Hunter & Port Stephens, New South Wales – land-based and boat / kayak. Calm, clear bay beach just inside the heads. Whiting and flathead on the sand, tailor at dawn off the rocks at Tomaree.

Depth & structure

Within about 450 m of Shoal Bay & Fingal Bay the seabed reaches about 6 m deep, with a drop-off from about 2 m to 5 m – drop-offs and holes like this are where fish hold, so fish the edges. Open the live forecast for the full depth map, deep holes and reefs (NSW Government 2018 survey).

Getting there

Parking along Shoal Bay Rd. Fingal Bay is just over the hill for surf and rocks.

Common questions

What fish can you catch at Shoal Bay & Fingal Bay?

Shoal Bay & Fingal Bay holds Sand Whiting, Dusky Flathead, Tailor, Yellowfin Bream, Squid, Australian Salmon and Snapper. Calm, clear bay beach just inside the heads. Whiting and flathead on the sand, tailor at dawn off the rocks at Tomaree.

What is the best bait at Shoal Bay & Fingal Bay?

Good baits here are Beach worms, Bloodworms, Live poddy mullet, Whole prawn, Pilchard on gang hooks, Garfish. For Sand Whiting, try Beach worms, Bloodworms, Live nippers (yabbies).

When is the best time to fish Shoal Bay & Fingal Bay?

The best months are usually Mar–Jul. On the day, dawn and dusk and the hours around the tide change are typically best. CastCall scores Shoal Bay & Fingal Bay every hour for the next two weeks using tides, wind, swell, water temperature and the moon.
