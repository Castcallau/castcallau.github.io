Eden – Twofold Bay Wharf fishing

A harbour and bay in Far South Coast, New South Wales – land-based and boat / kayak. Deep water wharves produce squid, trevally and salmon. Kings and snapper in Twofold Bay.

Depth & structure

Within about 450 m of Eden the seabed reaches about 13 m deep, with a drop-off from about 4 m to 13 m – drop-offs and holes like this are where fish hold, so fish the edges. Open the live forecast for the full depth map, deep holes and reefs (NSW Government 2018 survey).

Getting there

Main wharf and breakwall access in town. Ramp at Snug Cove.

Common questions

What fish can you catch at Eden?

Eden holds Australian Salmon, Squid, Dusky Flathead, Silver Trevally, Yellowtail Kingfish and Snapper. Deep water wharves produce squid, trevally and salmon. Kings and snapper in Twofold Bay.

What is the best bait at Eden?

Good baits here are Pilchard on gang hooks, Beach worms, Pilchard / mullet on a squid spike, Live yellowtail on a squid jag, Live poddy mullet, Whole prawn. For Australian Salmon, try Pilchard on gang hooks, Beach worms, Peeled prawn.

When is the best time to fish Eden?

The best months are usually Mar–Aug. On the day, dawn and dusk and the hours around the tide change are typically best. CastCall scores Eden every hour for the next two weeks using tides, wind, swell, water temperature and the moon.
