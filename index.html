CastCall – free NSW fishing forecast: best spots, times, tides and bait

CastCall is a free fishing forecast for New South Wales. It scores 45 saltwater and freshwater fishing spots every hour for the next three days, using tides, wind, swell, water temperature, barometric pressure, rain and river flow, sunrise and sunset, and the moon. It ranks the best spots, tells you the best time to go, sends fishing alerts and keeps a catch log.

Saltwater spots include Botany Bay, Sydney Harbour, Port Hacking, the Hawkesbury River, Long Reef, Pittwater, The Entrance, Norah Head, Lake Macquarie, Newcastle, Port Stephens, Forster, Port Macquarie, South West Rocks, Coffs Harbour, Yamba, Ballina, Wollongong, Bass Point, Jervis Bay, Batemans Bay, Narooma, Montague Island and Eden. Freshwater spots include the Nepean River, Lake Lyell, Lake Burrendong, Windamere Dam, Lake St Clair, Glenbawn Dam, the Clarence River, Lake Keepit, Copeton Dam, Lake Eucumbene, Lake Jindabyne, the Thredbo River, Burrinjuck Dam and Lake Mulwala.

Each spot shows which fish are biting – bream, dusky flathead, whiting, tailor, Australian salmon, mulloway (jewfish), luderick, yellowtail kingfish, snapper, squid, trevally, drummer, mahi mahi, mackerel, bonito, tiger flathead, Australian bass, golden perch (yellowbelly), Murray cod, trout, redfin and carp – plus the best bait, lures and rigs for each.

Stay safe: always check conditions yourself before fishing. Wear a lifejacket on rocks and in boats – it is the law at many NSW rock fishing spots and on small boats. Scores are an estimate from forecast models, not a guarantee. A NSW recreational fishing licence is needed in both salt and fresh water.
