    CastCall – free Australian fishing forecast: best spots, times, tides and bait

    CastCall is a free fishing forecast for all of Australia. It scores over 100 saltwater and freshwater fishing spots in every state every hour for the next two weeks, using tides, wind, swell, water temperature, barometric pressure, rain and river flow, sunrise and sunset, and the moon. Pin any spot on the map for an exact rundown. It ranks the best spots, tells you the best time to go, sends fishing alerts and keeps a catch log.

    Queensland: Gold Coast Seaway, Moreton Bay, Redcliffe, Noosa, K'gari (Fraser Island), Hervey Bay, the Whitsundays, Townsville, Cairns, the Great Barrier Reef and Lake Tinaroo. Northern Territory: Darwin Harbour, the Mary River, the Daly River and Dundee Beach. Western Australia: the Swan River, Perth beaches, Rottnest Island, Mandurah, Busselton Jetty, Albany, Esperance, Ningaloo and Broome. South Australia: Adelaide jetties, Port Lincoln, Kangaroo Island, Edithburgh, the Coorong and the Murray at Renmark. Victoria: St Kilda Pier, Port Phillip Bay, Western Port, Lakes Entrance, Mallacoota, Portland and Lake Eildon. Tasmania: the Derwent, Eaglehawk Neck, St Helens, the Tamar, Great Lake and Arthurs Lake. ACT: Lake Burley Griffin.

    New South Wales saltwater spots include Gymea Bay Baths, Yowie Bay, Lilli Pilli, Bundeena, Cronulla, Captain Cook Bridge, Tom Ugly's Bridge, Balmoral, Rose Bay, Ben Buckler, Homebush Bay, Terrigal, Kiama, Botany Bay, La Perouse, Brighton-le-Sands, Bate Bay, Sydney Harbour, Watsons Bay, Manly, Port Hacking, the Hawkesbury River, Long Reef, Pittwater, Brisbane Water, The Entrance, Norah Head, Lake Macquarie, Newcastle, Port Stephens, Shoal Bay, Crowdy Bay, Forster, Port Macquarie, South West Rocks, Coffs Harbour, Yamba, Ballina, Byron Bay, Wollongong, Bass Point, Jervis Bay, Batemans Bay, Narooma, Montague Island, Merimbula and Eden. NSW freshwater spots include the Nepean River, Lake Lyell, Lake Burrendong, Windamere Dam, Lake St Clair, Glenbawn Dam, the Clarence River, Lake Keepit, Copeton Dam, Lake Eucumbene, Lake Jindabyne, the Thredbo River, Burrinjuck Dam and Lake Mulwala.

    Each spot shows which fish are biting – bream, dusky flathead, whiting, tailor, Australian salmon, mulloway (jewfish), luderick, yellowtail kingfish, snapper, squid, trevally, drummer, mahi mahi, mackerel, bonito, tiger flathead, barramundi, mangrove jack, coral trout, threadfin salmon, queenfish, King George whiting, Australian herring (tommy ruff), black bream, WA dhufish, sand flathead, garfish, Australian bass, golden perch (yellowbelly), Murray cod, trout, redfin and carp – plus the best bait, lures and rigs for each.

    Stay safe: always check conditions yourself before fishing. Wear a lifejacket on rocks and in boats – it is the law at many rock fishing spots and on small boats. In the north, stay well back from the water: saltwater crocodiles live in rivers, estuaries and the sea. Scores are an estimate from forecast models, not a guarantee. Licences, size and bag limits, and closed seasons are different in every state.
