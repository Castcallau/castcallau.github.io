NSW fishing forecast – best spots, times, tides and bait

TideLine NSW is a free fishing forecast for New South Wales saltwater anglers. It scores 31 land-based and boat fishing spots every hour for the next three days, using tides, wind, swell, sea temperature, barometric pressure, rain, sunrise and sunset, and the moon. It then ranks the best spots and tells you the best time to go.

Spots include Botany Bay, Sydney Harbour, Port Hacking, the Hawkesbury River, Long Reef, Pittwater, The Entrance, Norah Head, Lake Macquarie, Newcastle, Port Stephens, Forster, Port Macquarie, South West Rocks, Coffs Harbour, Yamba, Ballina, Wollongong, Bass Point, Jervis Bay, Batemans Bay, Narooma, Montague Island and Eden.

Each spot shows which fish are biting – bream, dusky flathead, whiting, tailor, Australian salmon, mulloway (jewfish), luderick, yellowtail kingfish, snapper, squid, trevally, drummer, mahi mahi, mackerel, bonito and tiger flathead – plus the best bait, lures and rigs for each.

Stay safe: always check conditions yourself before fishing. Wear a lifejacket on rocks and in boats – it is the law at many NSW rock fishing spots and on small boats. Scores are an estimate from forecast models, not a guarantee.
